# Background Sources

Historical references, maps, photographs, texts, timelines, and other research may be stored here, or inside an adventure-specific `sources/` directory.

Catalog each item with:

- title and creator;
- publication or map date;
- file or stable link;
- provenance and usage rights, when known;
- relevant pages, sheets, or regions;
- intended use;
- confidence and known limitations.

Background sources provide facts and flavor. They do not supply Savage Worlds mechanics unless they are also declared as an authorized rules source.
