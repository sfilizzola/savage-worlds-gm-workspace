# Combat

## Round structure & initiative (p.91)
- A round is a full countdown of Action Cards, Ace to Deuce, with Jokers.
- Each Wild Card gets one Action Card/round (plus Edge bonuses); Extras usually share one card per group.
- Ties broken by suit: Spades > Hearts > Diamonds > Clubs.
- Joker drawn by a side: that side acts whenever it wants this round, and +2 to all Trait/damage rolls this round.

## Actions & movement (p.92-93)
- A character gets one action + normal movement on their turn for free; extra actions incur Multi-Action penalties (below).
- **Free actions**: short speech, dropping an item, falling prone, resisting a roll — no cost, several per turn allowed at GM discretion.
- **Limited actions**: one per turn max (e.g. Aim-like abilities), and separately one "limited free action" per turn.
- **Movement**: Pace inches/turn; Running adds the Running die (d6 default) at -2 to all actions that turn; each inch of climbing/crawling/swimming costs 2" of Pace; Difficult Ground costs 2"/inch.
- **Jumping**: 1" horizontal / 0.5" vertical as a free action (double with a 2"+ run-up); Athletics roll to extend.
- **Prone** (p.104): standing costs 2" of Pace; free action to drop prone.

## Melee attacks (p.93)
- TN to hit = target's **Parry** = `2 + half Fighting die type` (2 if no Fighting skill). See "Parry" below for full detail.

## Ranged attacks / Shooting (p.93-95, 97-98, 100-108)
- Shooting (or Athletics for thrown weapons) vs base **TN 4** at Short range.
- **Range penalties**: Short 0, Medium -2, Long -4, Extreme -8 (p.93). Extreme Range (up to 4x Long) requires **Aim** (below) and can't be used for thrown weapons.
- **Rate of Fire (RoF)**: number of Shooting dice rolled per action; assign hits among declared targets in any order, up to RoF (p.93).
- **Recoil**: firing RoF >1 in one action gives -2 to the Shooting roll unless negated (Rock and Roll! Edge, bipods, tripods, or firing fewer shots than max) (p.105).
- **Reloading**: single bullet/arrow/sling stone is a free action once/action; "Reload X" weapons need X actions; Running & Reloading requires an Agility roll at -2 (p.105).
- **Shotguns**: +2 Shooting at Short range only; damage 3d6 Short / 2d6 Medium / 1d6 Long; no Extreme range; double-barrel = roll damage once +4; slugs use 2d10 at any range (no shotgun bonus, Innocent Bystanders hit only on a 1) (p.105).
- **Wild Cards** add a Wild Die to Shooting and may assign it wherever it helps, capped at the weapon's RoF hits (p.93).
- **Ranged Weapons in Melee** (p.104): may only fire a power or a one-handed ranged weapon (pistol-sized, not "long arms") while engaged; TN is the defender's **Parry** instead of Short Range; firing at a non-adjacent target while in melee makes the attacker Vulnerable.
- **Innocent Bystanders** (p.102): a missed Shooting/thrown roll can hit an adjacent/in-line bystander on any die showing a 1 (RoF 2+ or shotgun: 1 or 2 hits a bystander); Wild Cards need their Wild Die to miss (except shotguns).
- **Off-Hand Attacks** (p.104): Shooting/Fighting with the off-hand alone is -2 unless Ambidextrous.
- **Suppressive Fire** (p.107): converts a rapid-firer (as fast as a revolver, no inter-shot reload) into an area attack. Uses 3x normal bullets for its RoF, always causes Recoil. Place a Medium Blast Template, single Shooting roll (RoF doesn't add dice here). Success = Distracted, raise = actually hit (no bonus damage). Max targets damaged = weapon's RoF; everyone else under the template is only Distracted.

## Damage & injury (p.93-96)
- Ranged weapons do fixed damage (e.g. a pistol 2d6); melee damage = Strength die + weapon die. Damage dice can Ace; a raise on the *attack* roll adds +1d6 bonus damage (all attack types).
- Damage >= Toughness -> **Shaken**; each raise beyond that inflicts a **Wound** (up to 3 Wounds for Wild Cards; 1 Wound Incapacitates an Extra).
- Shaken: only free actions until a Spirit roll (free action) succeeds, or a Benny is spent.
- Each Wound: -1 cumulative to Pace and all Trait rolls (max -3).
- **Incapacitated**: no actions; Vigor roll determines Injury Table result / Bleeding Out; **Soak rolls** (spend a Benny, Vigor check) can reduce Wounds from an attack before they're applied, and separately remove Shaken.
- **Healing**: Healing skill, 10 min/Wound level, -1 without a first aid kit; success removes 1 Wound, raise removes 2; only within the "Golden Hour" of the injury. Natural Healing: Vigor roll every 5 days (raise recovers 2).

## Called Shots (p.98)
- Modifier depends on target's Scale. Hand -4 (may Disarm), Head/Vitals -4 (+4 damage; -5 vs an open-faced helmet's face), Limbs -2 (no extra effect), Unarmored Area (?) bypasses Armor.

## Cover & Obstacles (p.99)
- Light -2 (1/4 obscured), Medium -4 (1/2 obscured or prone target), Heavy -6 (3/4 obscured), Near Total -8 (barely visible). If cover would have stopped an otherwise-successful hit, it acts as Armor equal to the Cover Bonus of the material (table p.99: heavy glass/drywall +2, sheet metal/steel door +4, oak door/cinder block +6, brick +8, stone/bulletproof glass/tree +10).

## Aim (p.97)
- Spend the entire turn (must be stationary) aiming a ranged weapon at one target; on your **next** turn only, ignore up to 4 points of Range/Cover/Called Shot/Scale/Speed penalties, or add +2 to the roll. Must be used on the first action of that next turn or it's lost.

## Area Effect Attacks (p.97-98)
- Blast Templates (Small 4yd/Medium 8yd/Large 12yd dia., Targets Affected 2/3/4) and Cone (18yd long, 3 targets)/Stream (24yd x2yd, 3 targets) Templates; ignore Dodge/Speed penalties per-target since they target a location. Roll Shooting (or Athletics for thrown/breath weapons) once for the template.
- **Deviation**: on a miss, thrown weapons deviate 1d6" (fired projectiles 2d6"), x2 at Medium range, x3 Long, x4 Extreme; roll a d12 as a clock face for direction.

## Parry
- **Definition & TN** (p.93): melee attackers' Target Number to hit you = `2 + half your Fighting die type` (2 if you have no Fighting skill). This is a static defense value, not a roll.
- **Ranged attacks do not use Parry** — they target the flat TN 4 (+ range/cover/etc.) instead, **except**: a ranged weapon fired at you while you're grappling/adjacent in melee uses your Parry as the TN instead of Short Range (p.104).
- **Prone** (p.104): a prone defender caught in melee has Parry -2 (and -2 to their own Fighting rolls).
- **Shields/Two Weapons**: a held shield's Parry bonus applies to Strength/Athletics Push rolls too (p.104); fighting with two melee weapons (or unarmed) adds +1 to Fighting (no bonus vs. Natural Weapons users) (p.104); off-hand weapons don't add their Parry bonus unless Ambidextrous (p.104).
- **Breaking Things** (p.98): to break a held object, the attacker's damage is compared to the wielder's Parry (or 2 if the object is motionless/unheld) vs. the object's Hardness.
- **Defend maneuver** (p.99) — the one case where a character *actively chooses* to invoke Parry: spend the whole turn defending (no Multi-Actions, may move but not run) for **+4 Parry** until the start of your next turn. Use it when surviving the round matters more than acting — badly outnumbered, buying time, protecting someone stationary, or waiting out a more favorable moment.
- **Tests vs. Parry** (p.108): when Fighting is used for a Test (not an actual attack), it's opposed by the target's **Agility**, not Parry — a Parry bonus does not help resist a Test.

## Other situational rules referenced
- **Desperate Attack** (p.100): +2/+4 Fighting, matching penalty to damage if it hits.
- **Disarm** (p.100): melee/ranged attack vs. the opponent's limb/hand (Called Shot, p.98); on a hit the defender resists with Strength or drops the item.
- **Distracted & Vulnerable** (p.100): Distracted = -2 all Trait rolls until end of next turn; Vulnerable = attacks against the target at +2 until end of their next turn (doesn't stack with The Drop — use the higher).
- **The Drop** (p.100): attacker catches a foe unaware/bound; +4 to attack and damage rolls for that one action; a Shaken-or-worse result forces a Vigor roll or the victim is knocked out (Knockout Blow).
- **Evasion** (p.100): attacks that must be "evaded" (flamethrowers, breath weapons) let an aware victim make an Agility roll at -2 to take no damage instead of resisting normally.
- **Fatigue** (p.100): Fatigued (-1 all Trait rolls) -> Exhausted (-2) -> Incapacitated; recovers 1 level/hour unless otherwise specified.
- **Ganging Up** (p.101): each additional adjacent attacker (not Stunned) adds +1 to Fighting rolls against a shared target, max +4; each adjacent ally of the defender cancels one point.
- **Grappling** (p.101): opposed Athletics; win = Entangled, raise = Bound; Scale difference subtracts from the (larger) attacker's total.
- **Hold** (p.102): delay your action within the round; interrupting another Held/acting character is an opposed Athletics roll.
- **Illumination** (p.102): Dim -2, Dark -4 (no target visible past 10"), Pitch Darkness -6 (may block sight-based powers).
- **Improvised Weapons** (p.102): count as armed but -2 to attack; Light/Medium/Heavy examples with Str+damage and range.
- **Mounted Combat** (p.103): rider uses the lower of Fighting/Riding; charging (6"+ straight approach) adds +4 damage; mounts follow Innocent Bystander-style rules for missed ranged attacks (hit on a 1).
- **Multi-Actions** (p.103): 2nd action = -2 to all actions that turn, 3rd action = -4 total; free actions are exempt.
- **Natural Weapons** (p.104): always considered armed (no Unarmed Defender bonus for foes); Bite requires a grapple; Claws +2 Athletics(climbing); Horns +4 damage after a Fighting action following a 5"+ run.
- **Nonlethal Damage** (p.104): fists/blunt weapons only (edged weapons -1 Fighting if used flat); Incapacitation = knocked out 1d6 hours instead of Wounds.
- **Push** (p.104): opposed Strength (or Athletics); success moves the foe 1" (2" with a raise, doubled if attacker's Scale is larger); defender may need an Athletics roll or falls prone.
- **Readying Weapons** (p.105): up to 2 items/turn free; more items or awkward retrieval cost an action.
- **Size & Scale** (p.106): Scale modifiers from Tiny (-6) to Gargantuan (+6); smaller-Scale attacker adds the difference to attack rolls against larger targets, larger attacker subtracts it.
- **Speed** (p.106): Relative Speed Penalty applies when attacker/target differ by 60+ MPH (-1) up to near-light speed (-10).
- **Stunned** (p.106): Distracted + Vulnerable + prone + can't act; recovers with a free-action Vigor roll at start of turn.
- **Support** (p.106-107): an ally rolls a relevant skill to add +1 (raise +2, max +4 total) to your Trait roll this round; Strength-based Support has no cap.
- **Surprise** (p.108): ambushers start on Hold; victims get a Notice roll to act normally, otherwise no Action Card that round.
- **Test** (p.108): using a skill to inflict Distracted/Vulnerable/other subjective effects on a foe instead of damage; resisted by the linked attribute (Fighting-as-Test is resisted by Agility, not Parry).
- **Touch Attack** (p.108): +2 Fighting when only trying to touch a foe (e.g. to deliver a power).
