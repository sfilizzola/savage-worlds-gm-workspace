# States (conditions)

Printed **State Summaries** live on p.203. Combat text that defines recovery and stacking is on the pages cited below. This file is the agent cache of those topics; the PDF remains authority.

## Distracted & Vulnerable (pp.100, 203)

- **Distracted:** −2 to all Trait rolls until the end of the victim’s next turn.
- **Vulnerable:** actions and attacks against the target are +2 until the end of their next turn. Does not stack with The Drop (p.100) — use the higher.

## Entangled & Bound (pp.98, 203)

- **Entangled:** cannot move; Vulnerable while Entangled.
- **Bound:** cannot move; Distracted and Vulnerable while Bound; no physical actions except trying to break free.
- **Break free:** action, Strength −2 or Athletics. Success: Entangled victim is free of one grappler/device. Bound victim improves to Entangled on a success, free on a raise.
- Device (net, cuffs): a failed break-free usually cannot be retried until the situation changes (GM’s call). Destroying the entanglement: see Breaking Things (p.98).

## Fatigue (pp.100, 203)

- **Fatigued:** −1 all Trait rolls. Another level → Exhausted.
- **Exhausted:** −2 all Trait rolls. Another level → Incapacitated.
- **Recovery:** unless the source says otherwise, improve one level per hour. Incapacitation from Fatigue: helpless, maybe unconscious (GM’s call) for 2d6 hours; if treatment is possible, a Healing roll can improve them to Exhausted (p.100). Mixed sources: drop one level when the shortest duration expires, then another when the longest expires.

## Injuries (pp.94–96, 106, 203)

- **Shaken (p.94):** only move and free actions (Running is allowed). Start of turn: Spirit as a free action to recover. Success → act normally. Benny removes Shaken instantly, even off-turn.
- **Stunned (p.106):** Distracted (until end of next turn), Vulnerable (until recovery from Stunned), prone, cannot move or act, does not count for Gang Up. Start of turn: Vigor as a free action. Success → no longer Stunned, remains Vulnerable until end of *next* turn. Raise → Vulnerable ends at the end of *this* turn.
- **Wounds (p.95):** each Wound −1 Pace (min 1″) and −1 Trait (max −3). Wild Cards take three, then further damage Incapacitates rather than adding a fourth Wound. Extras: one Wound Incapacitates (unless Resilient, p.178).
- **Incapacitation (p.95):** no actions; still dealt Action Cards. Immediate Vigor if Incapacitated by damage/injury:
  - Critical Failure → death.
  - Failure → Injury Table (permanent) and Bleeding Out.
  - Success → Injury Table until all Wounds heal.
  - Raise → Injury Table for 24 hours or until Wounds heal, whichever first.
- **Bleeding Out (p.95):** Vigor at the start of each turn (or each minute out of combat). Failure → dies. Success → roll again next interval. Raise → stable. Allies may stabilize with a Healing action (success).
- **Soak (p.96):** after damage, before Wounds apply, spend a Benny and roll Vigor (no Wound penalties from this hit yet). Each success and raise soaks one Wound from that attack. Soak all of them → also remove Shaken (even a prior Shaken). Once per attack; Bennies may reroll the Vigor.

Injury Table itself is p.95 (2d6: Unmentionables / Arm / Guts / Leg / Head). Do not paraphrase the table here unless a task needs a specific row.
