# Gear (modern firearms, modern armor, item Strength)

Lookup cache for equipping modern-era NPCs and PCs. Combat procedure for these weapons
(Recoil, Reloading, Shotguns, range penalties) is in `combat.md`; the page cites below are
the printed gear tables themselves.

## Minimum Strength (p.66)

Certain items list a Minimum Strength required to use without penalty.

- **Armor / worn gear:** each die-type difference between the character's Strength and the
  item's Minimum Strength inflicts **−1 to Pace (min 1″), Agility, and Agility-linked skill
  rolls**. Cumulative across multiple over-heavy items.
- Some items list d4 because Strength can be lower than d4.

**Shotgun damage bracket (p.105):** the `1–3d6` entry resolves as **3d6 at Short, 2d6 at
Medium, 1d6 at Long**, with **+2 to the Shooting roll at Short range only** (shot, not
slugs). At Medium and Long the shooter takes the ordinary range penalties with **no shotgun
bonus**. No Extreme Range. Slugs: no +2 bonus, 2d10 at any range, Extreme allowed. Double
barrels fired at one target: roll damage once, +4.

*Reading note.* The printed sentence runs "shotguns add +2 to the user's Shooting rolls and
cause 3d6 damage at Short Range, 2d6 at Medium, and 1d6 at Long," which can be parsed with
the +2 either scoped to Short Range alongside the 3d6 or unscoped. `combat.md` already
settled this workspace on **Short only**, and this file matches it. Keep the two files in
step; if the GM ever rules the other way, change both.

## Modern armor (p.70)

**Ballistic Protection:** armor marked `*` **reduces damage from bullets by 4** (bullets
includes physical shot from a firearm). AP applies only to the item's actual Armor value.

| Item | Armor | Min Str | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thick Coat, Leather Jacket | +1 | d4 | torso, arms |
| Leather Riding Chaps | +1 | d4 | legs |
| Kevlar Riding Jacket | +2 | d4 | torso, arms |
| Kevlar Riding Jeans | +2 | d4 | legs |
| Bike helmet | +2 | d4 | head |
| Motorcycle Helmet | +3 | d4 | head |
| Flak Jacket (Vietnam-era) | +2 | d6 | torso |
| **Kevlar Vest** | **+2\*** | **d6** | torso |
| Kevlar Vest with ceramic inserts | +4* | d8 | torso |
| Kevlar helmet | +4* | d4 | head |

A d4-Strength character in a Kevlar Vest (Min Str d6) is one die type short and therefore
takes −1 Pace / Agility / Agility-linked skills (p.66).

## What core does *not* cover

SWADE core has **no entry for bulk commercial explosives** — no dynamite, blasting powder,
det cord, or blasting caps. The only printed bulk-blast gear is military: **grenades
(p.78)** and **mines (p.79)**, all tagged Heavy Weapon and resolved with Blast Templates
(Area Effect Attacks, pp.97–98). The **Repair** skill "covers the use of demolitions and
explosives" (p.34) but supplies no yield or damage value. A scenario needing civilian or
degraded explosives has no printed authority and requires a GM ruling.
