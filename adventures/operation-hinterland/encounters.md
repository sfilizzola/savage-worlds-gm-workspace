# Encounters and Challenges

Use an encounter only when the fiction puts these people in play. This is a hollow garrison: named officers are the fuse, not a hidden army. No occult, no SS templates, no Sanity.

Opposition is rebuilt from Weird War II Chapter 9 against SWADE. Old WW2 Edges that do not exist in SWADE, or that WW2 itself withholds from NPCs, are not used.

**Shared conversion (all blocks below)**

```text
Authority: SWADE Fifth Printing (2023), pp. 87–88 (Wild Cards & Extras);
  p. 95 (Extras Incapacitated by one Wound); p. 89 (GM Bennies may be
  spent on non-Wild Cards); p. 29 (Athletics includes throwing;
  Knowledge (Battle) → Battle); p. 31 (Charisma removed; Throwing → Athletics);
  pp. 44–45 (Leadership Edges affect allied Extras only unless Natural Leader);
  p. 65 (Armor hits the torso unless a Called Shot); p. 66 (Snapfire −2 if
  the shooter moved);   p. 93 (grenades use Athletics); pp. 98–99 (Called Shot
  head/vitals −4, +4 damage); p. 102 (Illumination); p. 202 (do not build Extras with PC creation)
Setting: Weird War II p. 14 (steel helmet Armor +2); p. 21 (Rank Edge
  extra Benny / +2 Toughness — "Non-player characters ... do not get
  these additional abilities"); pp. 144–145 (Wehrmacht Officer / Infantry /
  Volkssturm); p. 152 (German small arms / Stielhandgranate)
House rule: none
Summary: Templates converted. Rank is flavor. Helmets use RUL-2026-08-29-01.
Applied here: all opposition
```

**Helmets (ruled):** Armor +2 vs Called Shots to the head. No 50% save. Targeting the face of an open-faced helmet is −5 and bypasses the helmet (SWADE pp. 98–99).

**Night light (SWADE p.102; BG-OH-009):** default **Dark (−4)** — typical night; targets not visible past 10″ (20 yards). 6 January 1945 is Last Quarter (moon up around midnight). First half of the night stays Dark. After moonrise, a *clear* snow sky or a lamp/stove can be **Dim (−2)**. Pitch Darkness (−6) only in an unlit cellar or sealed room. Cloud that night is unknown.

**Cold:** if they linger exposed, use Hazards (SWADE p.125). Do not apply a hidden Fatigue at the LZ.

## Wörnitztor watch (2 Extras)

- **Trigger:** the four close the east gate, make noise there, or try to talk past the stove.
- **Purpose and stakes:** nearest door from the LZ. A botch is noise and a hunt seed, not a failed mission.
- **Opposition goal:** get through the night. Challenge a loud close. They will not sortie as a fortress.
- **PC objective:** pass, bypass, or silence without lighting the fuse if they can help it.
- **Environment and tactical facts:** gate passage, gatehouse, ice, short view along the east walk toward Altrathausplatz. Break-contact is available. Civilians in nearby houses.
- **Scaling:** two Extras for 4 Seasoned. Do not add a squad.
- **Early-removal risk:** mitigated. First violence has an out (break contact, civilians, thin watch). A single grab or shot should not bench a player.
- **Failure consequence:** shout, then minutes, then Keller or a runner. Time and noise. Postern / circuit / climb still exist.
- **Exit conditions:** they are talked past, slipped, dropped, or the four leave the gate.

### Runtime statistics

**Volkssturm / convalescent Extra** (use for both). WW2 p.144: Volkssturm are always Novice; d4 in most skills; Spirit usually d4.

- Extra. Pace 6; Parry 4; Toughness 5
- Attributes: Agility d6, Smarts d6, Spirit d4, Strength d6, Vigor d6
- Skills: Athletics d4, Fighting d4, Notice d4, Shooting d4, Stealth d4
- Edges / Hindrances: none
- Gear: Kar 98K (one may have only a P38 or no rifle), greatcoat, maybe a helmet. Stove, lamp.

One Wound Incapacitates (SWADE p.95). Aftermath Vigor if you care who lived (p.96).

### Rules verification

- SWADE: pp. 87–88, 95, 202
- Setting: WW2 p.144 Volkssturm note; p.152 Kar 98K
- Adaptation: Throwing d4 → Athletics d4. No Charisma. Not Experienced Wehrmacht (Dodge / Rock and Roll! omitted — late-war hollow watch).

### Difficulty review

- Expected pressure: low if the four are quiet or talk. Medium if they stand in the passage and shoot.
- Volatility: two rifles can still drop a Seasoned PC on a raise. Telegraph it.
- Resource drain: noise more than Bennies.
- Player count: written for 4. Do not add bodies if someone is late.

## Dummy site — Hofmann (and Stein if woken)

- **Trigger:** they enter or make noise at the Rathaus.
- **Purpose and stakes:** leftover pays (east wire, books toward Nördlinger). Dummy wreck is not a win.
- **Opposition goal:** Hofmann wants the night to pass. Stein wants order in *his* building if woken.
- **PC objective:** read that this is not the exchange; get a vector; leave or deal with whoever they woke.
- **Environment and tactical facts:** offices, stair, blackout, side door. Not a dungeon. One lamp. Stein’s quarters are nearby (Weinmarkt), not in the exchange.
- **Scaling:** Hofmann alone if quiet. Stein (Wild Card) if loud or late. Do not fill the square with a platoon.
- **Early-removal risk:** mitigated. Capture here is same-night recoverable (Voss / Benoit).
- **Failure consequence:** fuse starts; leftover still exists (wire, tag, empty board room).
- **Exit conditions:** talk, freeze, flight, a short fight, or they leave with a tell that the aerial or a line still lives.

### Runtime statistics

**Gefr. Lina Hofmann** — Extra, clerk leftover. Not the only vector. World rule: gender is not a question.

- Extra. Pace 6; Parry 4; Toughness 5
- Attributes: Agility d6, Smarts d6, Spirit d6, Strength d6, Vigor d6
- Skills: Athletics d4, Common Knowledge d6, Fighting d4, Notice d6, Persuasion d6, Shooting d4, Stealth d4
- Edges / Hindrances: none
- Gear: clerk papers, lamp. A P38 may be in a drawer, not in her hand at start.

**Hptm. Friedrich Stein** — Wild Card. Ortskommandant. The fuse. Converted from Wehrmacht Officer + Experienced package (WW2 p.144): Smarts / Battle / Persuasion raised; Command; Hold the Line! Dodge omitted (staff officer, not a street fighter). Rank (Officer) extra Benny **not** applied (WW2 p.21, NPCs).

- Wild Card. Pace 6; Parry 5; Toughness 5 (helmet +2 vs head Called Shots)
- Attributes: Agility d6, Smarts d8, Spirit d6, Strength d6, Vigor d6
- Skills: Athletics d4, Battle d8, Fighting d6, Notice d6, Persuasion d8, Shooting d6, Stealth d4
- Edges: Command (SWADE p.45), Hold the Line! (p.45 — Extras in Command Range +1 Toughness)
- Hindrances: none required. Prussian Code of Honor is optional flavor (WW2 p.144), not a lock.
- Gear: P38, binoculars if he dressed, steel helmet if he had time, greatcoat. Staff sidearm unless the hunt is already on.
- Bennies: 2 as a GM Wild Card when he appears (SWADE p.89), plus the common GM pool.

Hold the Line! is kept (GM, 2026-08-29): once he is directing a hunt, extras in Command Range (5″ / 10 yards) get +1 Toughness. They are still Extras. There is no off-map battalion.

### Rules verification

- SWADE: pp. 44–45, 87–89, 95, 202
- Setting: WW2 pp. 144 (officer / Experienced), 21 (Rank NPC clause), 152 (P38)
- Adaptation: Knowledge (Battle) → Battle. Charisma dropped. Rank Edge bonuses omitted.

### Difficulty review

- Hofmann alone: talk / scare, not a fight.
- Stein woken: one Wild Card plus whoever arrives in minutes. Four Seasoned should win a straight fight and pay in noise.
- Volatility: Command / Hold the Line! matter only after extras are in his 5″ (10 yd) range.
- Resource drain: time and the fuse, not a attrition map.

## Keller’s night round (north wall)

- **Trigger:** they are seen on the north wall-walk, at Grüner Turm, or a shout pulls her off her stretch.
- **Purpose and stakes:** night duty / deputy. She orders the thin watch. She is a possible loft sighting, not the key to the tower.
- **Opposition goal:** the night watch does its job. Escalate a hunt. Not a side change.
- **PC objective:** avoid, talk, drop, or use her movement as a clock. Capturing her is optional.
- **Environment and tactical facts:** wall-walk between Rothenburger Tor and Grüner Turm at start. Ice. She can reach the east face later if they linger.
- **Scaling:** Keller alone at first contact. A pair of extras only after the fuse, not a company.
- **Early-removal risk:** mitigated at first sighting (break contact along the walk). After lethality is on, she can kill.
- **Failure consequence:** hunt starts; loft and board remain findable without her.
- **Exit conditions:** she passes without seeing them; a short clash; she runs for Stein or the loft.

### Runtime statistics

**Oblt. Marta Keller** — Wild Card. Converted from Wehrmacht Officer (WW2 p.144) with field-watch extras: Notice raised, Combat Reflexes. Rank Benny omitted. World rule: gender is not a question.

- Wild Card. Pace 6; Parry 5; Toughness 5 (helmet +2 vs head)
- Attributes: Agility d6, Smarts d6, Spirit d6, Strength d6, Vigor d6
- Skills: Athletics d6, Battle d6, Fighting d6, Notice d8, Persuasion d6, Shooting d6, Stealth d6
- Edges: Command (p.45), Combat Reflexes (p.41 — +2 to recover from Shaken)
- Gear: P38 at start (staff sidearm). After the fuse she may pick up an MP40. Steel helmet, binoculars, greatcoat, whistle or lamp.
- Bennies: 2 when she appears (p.89).

She does **not** have Hold the Line! — that sits on Stein so the hunt hardens when *he* is directing it.

### Rules verification

- SWADE: pp. 41, 45, 87–89
- Setting: WW2 p.144 officer; p.152 P38 / MP40
- Adaptation: Knowledge (Battle) → Battle. Rank bonuses omitted. Experienced Dodge / Hold the Line! not stacked on both officers.

### Difficulty review

- One Wild Card on ice is a fight they can take or skip.
- Volatility: Combat Reflexes keeps her in play a round longer than Hofmann.
- If a pair arrives with her after the fuse, still a local hunt.

## Switchboard — 2 operators (Altrathausplatz)

- **Trigger:** they enter or cut the live board.
- **Purpose and stakes:** one of three heads. Two extras. Brandt is **not** here.
- **Opposition goal:** keep the board useful or get out and scream.
- **PC objective:** kill this head (smash, cut, power, fire, remove operators). Fire is a civilian problem.
- **Environment and tactical facts:** interior, cables, back door to the lanes. Low winter traffic. A runner only after trouble.
- **Scaling:** two Extras. Do not add Brandt to this room.
- **Early-removal risk:** mitigated if they come in quiet. A panicked Auto burst (MP40) is the swing — telegraph it.
- **Failure consequence:** board wounded or still up; other heads remain; possible landline scream if loft and papers live.
- **Exit conditions:** board dead, operators fled / down / talking, or the four break off.

### Runtime statistics

**Signals operator Extra** (×2). No Nachrichten template in WW2; built as Extras (SWADE p.202) on the late-war Wehrmacht infantry chassis with Fighting/Athletics dropped and Electronics added.

- Skills: Athletics d4, Electronics d6, Fighting d4, Notice d6, Repair d4, Shooting d6, Stealth d4
- Gear: one P38 each; one of the two may have an MP40 on a hook. No squad MG.

### Rules verification

## Hunt extras (after the fuse)

Use when Stein or Keller has lit a local hunt. Still a hollow town.

- **Trigger:** noise, a believed witness, a runner, or dawn plus a living path.
- **Purpose and stakes:** closing pressure. Out-of-town help needs a living hydra (or landline) **and** time.
- **Opposition goal:** find the four, restore order, protect whatever head still lives.
- **PC objective:** finish a second head and/or leave.
- **Environment and tactical facts:** short streets, civilians, ice. Pairs on lanes, not a battalion.
- **Scaling:** start with **one pair** of Volkssturm / convalescent extras (same block as Wörnitztor). Add Keller and/or Stein when the fiction puts them there. Do not empty the map of extra bodies from other pins — move them.
- **Early-removal risk:** after they have acted in town, lethality is on. Communicate it.
- **Failure consequence:** costly exit, a PC down, a bag leaving. Mission result is already decided or still decidable in flight.
- **Exit conditions:** they are out; they are hidden; a head dies in flight; they are taken (same-night chair still exists).

If Stein is within Command Range, Hold the Line! applies to those extras. Command helps them recover from Shaken / Stunned (SWADE p.45). Natural Leader is **not** on either officer, so Leadership Edges do not buff the player Wild Cards.

### Difficulty review

- Expected pressure: rising, still local.
- Volatility: four Seasoned vs two extras is still favored; vs two extras + a Wild Card officer it is a real fight.
- Do not add Experienced Wehrmacht (Dodge, Rock and Roll!, MG42 teams). That would be a different garrison than the one locked in `locations.md`.

## Extraction vehicles

- **Trigger:** Story Point 6, or earlier if a player remembers a sighting from SP1, SP2, or SP4.
- **Purpose and stakes:** three concrete, non-required exits. Full location/state/capacity/risk table: `locations.md` "Extraction vehicles."
- **Opposition goal:** n/a — props, not NPCs.
- **PC objective:** optional faster or higher-capacity exit; leaving on foot by any gate still completes the mission.
- **Environment and tactical facts:** Stein's Kübelwagen (Rathaus lean-to, near-full tank, no key in it); loft dispatch motorcycle-and-sidecar (Grüner Turm base, keys on it, loud, seats 2); Opel Blitz cargo truck (Wörnitztor supply yard, battery pulled for winter, quarter tank, best capacity).
- **Scaling:** no stat blocks needed; these are not combatants.
- **Failure consequence:** a failed `Repair` roll (Kübelwagen without its key, or the Blitz's cold start) costs a few minutes and engine noise — not the vehicle itself, and not a bench.
- **Exit conditions:** used, ignored, or left running/abandoned; none of the three is required for any end state.

### Rules verification

- SWADE: p.34 (Repair; the skill already covers demolitions for this team, reused here for a cold engine)
- Setting: none — this is a mechanical prop, not a WW2 vehicle-combat subsystem
- Adaptation: no chase mechanics. A long vehicle chase is a named pacing cut elsewhere in this adventure; these vehicles exist to shorten an exit, not to start one.
