# Hptm. Friedrich Stein

- **Canon status:** proposed
- **Visibility:** mixed (named at briefing; met only if woken, loud, or the hunt is his)
- **Role:** Ortskommandant. The fuse. Not a hidden army and not the objective.
- **Current objective:** order in his town and in his building. No surprise on his watch.
- **Fear:** looking like the man who lost a quiet rear post in the night.
- **Leverage/resources:** the thin watch, Keller as night duty, a staff sidearm, the Rathaus as *his* office. He knows Brandt holds the books. He does not have a battalion to teleport.
- **Secret:** the Rathaus is not the exchange. He still treats it as his house. He knows the net lives elsewhere (board east, loft north, books with Brandt). He does not know the Allied raid plan.
- **Relationships:** Keller runs the night watch; he expects her to do that job. Brandt is the signals NCO, not a drinking friend. Hofmann is leftover paper in his building. Civilians are his town, not a puzzle.
- **If ignored:** the fuse stays unlit until dawn or a clear insult. He stays in quarters on or just off Weinmarkt unless noise or a runner fetches him.
- **What can change their behavior:** noise he believes, a witness, a call from the hydra, someone in his house. Capture, a deal, or a hunt that is already lost can make him escalate, stall, or fail to — not switch sides.

## Portrayal

Write as a person with limited knowledge, a current objective, and a fear. Lines they would actually say in this moment. Not exposition, not a briefing, not the narrator's voice.

- First impression: woken — coat over night clothes, or dressed if they were loud long enough. Staff, not a street fighter. The square is his even when the board is not.
- Voice/manner cue: German. Tight. He asks who is in *his* house before he explains the war.
- Spoken lines: one to three quoted lines the GM can use at the table.
  - Woken at the Rathaus: `"Das ist noch immer mein Gebäude. Wer ist in meinem Haus?" (This is still my building. Who is in my house?)`
  - If he is directing the hunt: `"Hebt ab, wer noch antwortet." (Raise whoever still answers.)`
- Avoid: reciting the hydra rule for the players; SS fortress talk; English slogans; treating the four as the only thing that exists in Franconia.
- Pressure variants (one short line or action-plus-line each; same person, not a second personality):
  - Cooperate: a narrow answer if they have leverage or he wants them *out*. `"Die Bücher hat Brandt. Nicht hier." (Brandt has the books. Not here.)` He does not draw them a raid map.
  - Refuse: `"Raus aus meinem Haus." (Out of my house.)`
  - Threatened: he does not speechify. He wants the watch. `"Wache. Sofort." (Watch. Now.)`
  - Ignored: he stays off the floor. No speech to an empty Weinmarkt. Fuse unlit until dawn or a clear insult.
