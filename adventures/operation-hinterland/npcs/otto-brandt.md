# Fw. Otto Brandt

- **Canon status:** proposed
- **Visibility:** GM-only until found (the briefing names books, not this man)
- **Role:** Signals NCO. The books head is his satchel, not his death. Not the only path to the papers or the plot.
- **Current objective:** papers stay useful and in German hands.
- **Fear:** being the man who lost the traffic.
- **Leverage/resources:** the live books and last traffic, a P38, a street door on Nördlinger Straße, routes toward a gate, the board, a stove, or Stein’s office.
- **Secret:** he knows the Rathaus is not the exchange, the board is on Altrathausplatz, the loft is the north tower. He does not know the four’s mission until they are in his room.
- **Relationships:** Stein and Keller know he holds the books; they are not standing over him. Operators can shout for him. Hofmann files leftover paper; she is not his clerk tonight.
- **If ignored:** papers stay in play. He sleeps or keeps the satchel in the room. At dawn or alarm he may walk to the exchange. The hydra stays useful even after a smashed set elsewhere.
- **What can change their behavior:** alarm, dawn, a runner from the Rathaus, someone in the billet. Capture, a deal, or flight with the bag. If he burns the books himself, give a sign (smoke, empty drawer). He need not die.

## Portrayal

Write as a person with limited knowledge, a current objective, and a fear. Lines they would actually say in this moment. Not exposition, not a briefing, not the narrator's voice.

- First impression: commandeered room, stove, cot, a satchel or drawer. One NCO, not a staff HQ.
- Voice/manner cue: German. Flat. The bag is the point. He does not speechify if he runs.
- Spoken lines: one to three quoted lines the GM can use at the table.
  - `"Die Bücher bleiben bei mir." (The books stay with me.)`
  - If he runs: he moves the satchel toward a gate, the board, a stove, or Stein’s office. No speech to the street.
- Avoid: explaining the hydra rule; a boss monologue; putting him on Altrathausplatz at start; making the bag vanish with no sign.
- Pressure variants (one short line or action-plus-line each; same person, not a second personality):
  - Cooperate: `"You want a look. Here. Then they go back in the bag."` He does not gift the loft and the board as a tour unless they already have him and the leverage.
  - Refuse: `"Nein. Das ist nicht euer Posten." (No. This is not your post.)`
  - Threatened: he does not speechify; he moves. If he speaks: `"Schieß und du hast immer noch keine Bücher, die du lesen kannst." (Shoot and you still don’t have books you can read.)`
  - Ignored: he keeps the satchel. Papers stay in play. No speech to an empty stove.
