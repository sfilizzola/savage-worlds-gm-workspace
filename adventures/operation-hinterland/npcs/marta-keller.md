# Oblt. Marta Keller

- **Canon status:** proposed
- **Visibility:** mixed (named at briefing; a possible sighting on the north wall, not the key to the loft)
- **Role:** Night duty / deputy. Orders the thin watch. One half of the fuse.
- **Current objective:** the night watch does its job. Her stretch stays quiet.
- **Fear:** a hole in the wall-walk on her round that she has to explain to Stein.
- **Leverage/resources:** the north walk (Rothenburger Tor to Grüner Turm), whistle or lamp, binoculars, P38; MP40 only after the fuse. She can pull a pair later. She cannot fill the map.
- **Secret:** she knows who holds the books and that the loft is on her stretch. She does not know the Allied pack. Gender is not a table question.
- **Relationships:** Stein is her Ortskommandant; she escalates when he orders it or when she hears the noise first. The loft watch is on her wall, not her friend. Street watch wants the night to pass; she wants them awake enough.
- **If ignored:** thin posts stay sleepy. She continues the slow north round. She is not sitting in Rothenburger Tor. She may reach the east wall later if the four linger, not at the first minute on the LZ.
- **What can change their behavior:** a sighting on the walk, a shout, Stein’s order, a live loft in trouble. She escalates the hunt. She does not change sides.

## Portrayal

Write as a person with limited knowledge, a current objective, and a fear. Lines they would actually say in this moment. Not exposition, not a briefing, not the narrator's voice.

- First impression: greatcoat, ice, a lamp or binoculars, walking a stretch that should be empty. Duty, not a speech.
- Voice/manner cue: German. Field, not staff. Short orders. She does not explain the tower.
- Spoken lines: one to three quoted lines the GM can use at the table.
  - If she sees them on the wall: `"Die Wache ist heute Nacht meine. Runter von der Mauer." (The watch is mine tonight. Off the wall.)`
  - If the hunt is hers: `"Paar auf die Gassen. Ausgangssperre. Holt sie." (Pair on the lanes. Curfew. Get them.)`
- Avoid: handing Grüner Turm as a quest pin; English slogans; a company on the walk; making her the only way to the aerial.
- Pressure variants (one short line or action-plus-line each; same person, not a second personality):
  - Cooperate: only if she has a reason (wrong uniforms she still buys, a better threat than Stein). `"Off the wall. Then you talk to me below."` She does not tour the loft.
  - Refuse: `"Bleibt stehen. Papiere." (Stay where you are. Papers.)`
  - Threatened: whistle first, then movement toward Stein or the loft. If she speaks: `"Eine auf der Mauer—" (One on the wall—)`
  - Ignored: she walks on. Thin posts stay sleepy. No speech to empty ice.
