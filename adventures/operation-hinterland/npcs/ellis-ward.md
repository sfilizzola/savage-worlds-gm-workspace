# Capt. Ellis Ward

- **Canon status:** proposed
- **Visibility:** player-known
- **Role:** U.S. intel/ops. Briefs Operation Hinterland in Pont-à-Mousson. Does not jump.
- **Current objective:** get the four in the air tonight with the pack he believes.
- **Fear:** they sit in the schoolroom arguing the map until the strip is gone.
- **Leverage/resources:** the November sheet, the folder, the truck, the clock. He cannot name live pins he does not have.
- **Secret:** none he is keeping. He believes the Rathaus is the exchange. It is a false head. He does not know they will land east.
- **Relationships:** the four are his lift, not his friends. Stein and Keller are names in the folder, not men he has met. Voss missed this lift; Ward does not wait for him.
- **If ignored:** he closes the folder, puts them on the truck, and is off-map after the jump. He does not follow, radio a correction, or appear in Dinkelsbühl.
- **What can change their behavior:** a refused kit ask; they dawdle. He will skip chat and move the clock. He will not invent a better pin.

## Portrayal

Write as a person with limited knowledge, a current objective, and a fear. Lines they would actually say in this moment. Not exposition, not a briefing, not the narrator's voice.

- First impression: rear-echelon coat, folder already open, stove heat, no shells on the roof. Calm because he thinks the pack is enough.
- Voice/manner cue: American, clipped, sure. He marks while he talks. He does not perform a code.
- Spoken lines: one to three quoted lines the GM can use at the table.
  - “That’s the pack. That’s what you jump with.”
  - “The Rathaus is the exchange. The aerial is a second head. You find it in town.”
  - If they dawdle: “The strip won’t wait on a better map.”
  - Full four briefing beats (hydra rule, map marks, Stein and Keller named, no second mission): `RUN.md` Story Point 0. Do not ad-lib the east LZ or any live head.
- Avoid: live pins (Altrathausplatz, Grüner Turm, Brandt); admitting the Rathaus might be wrong; a fight in this room; a second mission.
- Pressure variants (one short line or action-plus-line each; same person, not a second personality):
  - Cooperate: he answers a kit or weather ask from what a rear officer actually has. “Ordinary extras. That’s what the shed has. Then the truck.”
  - Refuse: they want a room that kills the hydra, or a pin he does not have. “That’s the pack. That’s what you jump with.”
  - Threatened: this is not a combat situation. He does not draw. If the table goes somewhere it should not: he steps back, folder shut. “You’re not starting it in this room.”
  - Ignored: he checks his watch, points at the door, and they are on the truck. Off-map after the jump. No speech to an empty classroom.
