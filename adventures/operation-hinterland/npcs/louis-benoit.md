# Pvt. Louis Benoit

- **Canon status:** proposed
- **Visibility:** GM-only until found
- **Role:** Free French evader. Re-entry and an extra pair of eyes. Not a required guide. Not a unique clue. Not a combat solution.
- **Current objective:** stay hidden and alive under the east square.
- **Fear:** the cellar going hot — a hunt, a neighbor, armed strangers on the stair.
- **Leverage/resources:** a hole to hide in, stolen food, civilian clothes, maybe a P38 with almost no ammunition. French d8; German d4. He has heard the east quarter, not the Allied briefing.
- **Secret:** he may have heard a drop or only the town. He can know the east quarter is where signals people live and work, and that the Rathaus is officers, not the board. Anything else is extra, not load-bearing.
- **Relationships:** not Hofmann, not Brandt, not Ward’s lift. Neighbors may or may not know he is there. Prefer **Cpl. Pieter Voss** if a player needs a finished Seasoned sheet.
- **If ignored:** he stays in the cellar. He does not come looking for the four or walk them to the heads.
- **What can change their behavior:** they find him; a hunt; a player needs a same-night chair. He can run, hide, come with them, or be captured as a complication. He will not tour every head unless the table makes that true.

## Portrayal

Write as a person with limited knowledge, a current objective, and a fear. Lines they would actually say in this moment. Not exposition, not a briefing, not the narrator's voice.

- First impression: cold cellar, stolen food, no uniform on a hanger, a man who has been hiding. Pitch Darkness (−6) if unlit.
- Voice/manner cue: French first. English only if he thinks they are Allied and he has to. German is thin and he will not fake a sentry.
- Spoken lines: one to three quoted lines the GM can use at the table.
  - If they find him: `"Ne tirez pas. Français." (Don’t shoot. French.)`
  - If they want a guide: `"Je ne marche pas en ville. Je reste en dessous." (I don’t walk the town. I stay under it.)`
- Avoid: a required tour of board, loft, and books; combat heroics; turning him into the only intel; a unique biography in the first shout.
- Pressure variants (one short line or action-plus-line each; same person, not a second personality):
  - Cooperate: extra eyes, not a map. `"East. That’s where they work. Not the Rathaus."` Then he wants to go back under.
  - Refuse: he stays in the dark. `"Non. I stay here."`
  - Threatened: hands up, then he runs for another hole if he can. `"Ne tirez pas—"` He does not bargain with hydra pins he does not have.
  - Ignored: he stays hidden. No speech up through the floor.
