# Gefr. Lina Hofmann

- **Canon status:** proposed
- **Visibility:** GM-only until found
- **Role:** Late clerk leftover at the Rathaus. One vector, never the only one. Dummy-site paper, not the exchange.
- **Current objective:** get through the night at the lamp and the stove.
- **Fear:** being the body in the doorway when someone louder arrives — Stein, a hunt, or strangers with guns.
- **Leverage/resources:** leftover files, a lamp, a stove, old paper. A P38 may be in a drawer, not in her hand at start. She knows *work* went east and that the Feldwebel has the books toward Nördlinger. She names the north tower only if they ask.
- **Secret:** this house is not the board. She files here. She does not run the net and does not know the Allied raid. Gender is not a table question.
- **Relationships:** Stein still treats this as *his* office; she does not want him woken. Brandt is the Feldwebel with the books, not a man in this room. Keller is a name on the night watch, not at this desk.
- **If ignored:** she stays at the lamp. The dummy site still pays by wire, tag, empty board room, and habit. She is not a required conversation.
- **What can change their behavior:** found, questioned, or frightened. She may talk, freeze, or point at leftover work. If she dies or clams, leftover still exists.

## Portrayal

Write as a person with limited knowledge, a current objective, and a fear. Lines they would actually say in this moment. Not exposition, not a briefing, not the narrator's voice.

- First impression: one lamp on the ground floor, cold, ink, a clerk who wanted the night to pass. No aerial on this roof. No machine hum.
- Voice/manner cue: quiet German. Tired. She talks about *her* paper and *their* cut-over, not the players’ mission.
- Spoken lines: one to three quoted lines the GM can use at the table.
  - If she talks: `"Die Vermittlung ist nicht hier. Die haben umgelegt. Ostzimmer. Ich mache Papier. Ich fahre das Netz nicht." (The exchange isn’t here. They cut it over. East rooms. I do paper. I don’t run the net.)`
  - If they ask about a tower, a radio, or why the building is dead: `"Der Nordturm redet noch. Das ist nicht dieses Haus." (The north tower still talks. That’s not this house.)`
  - If they press who has the traffic: `"Die Bücher hat der Feldwebel. Richtung Nördlinger. Das ist sein Posten." (The Feldwebel has the books. Toward Nördlinger. That’s his post.)` Raise or leverage can get the name **Brandt**.
- Avoid: the hydra rule; a slogan dump of all three live pins in one breath; a unique life story; English “as you know.”
- Pressure variants (one short line or action-plus-line each; same person, not a second personality):
  - Cooperate: `"You want a look. East rooms. Then I go back to the stove."`
  - Refuse: `"Nein. Das ist nicht euer Posten." (No. This is not your post.)`
  - Threatened: she freezes or points. Voice gone small. `"Ost. Nur ost." (East. Only east.)` She does not become a raid brief.
  - Ignored: she stays at the lamp and files. Dummy leftover still exists without her. No speech to an empty office.
