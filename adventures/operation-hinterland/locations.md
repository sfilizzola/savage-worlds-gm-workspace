## Commandeered schoolroom — Pont-à-Mousson

- **Role in adventure:** table start. Calm briefing. First small PC-to-PC beats. Not the opening trouble.
- **First impression:**
  - **Climate:** Stove heat in a winter room. Coats still on. No shells on the roof.
  - **See:** Commandeered classroom. Blackout paper. November sheet. Kit. Ward.
  - **Hear:** The stove. A quiet street beyond the paper — not a front.
  - **Feel:** Calm. Rear-area. The pressure is the clock, not incoming fire.
- **Current situation:** **Capt. Ellis Ward** believes the pack. The four have not jumped. Kit is on the table. Optional: a short ask around the base (quartermaster / shed) for ordinary extras, then the truck to a night strip.
- **Environment:** one room, then a short walk or truck. Not a second map. Not a shopping scene.
- **People/forces present:** Ward; the four. Voss is not here. No combat.
- **What can be learned:** Operation Hinterland; night of 6–7 January; Nordwind is the clock, Dinkelsbühl is the target (signals station still talking toward Alsace); November sheet: walls, east water (Wörnitz), four gates; planned DZ west / Segringer Tor; thin rear garrison, not a fortress; the station is a hydra, not one shack; three **types** (board / aerial / books); cut two and it dies (one still talks); Rathaus named as the **exchange** HQ has; find the aerial and the books on the ground; Stein and Keller named as responsible, not the objective; no second mission; extraction after two heads are off. Ward cannot name the live pins. Full spoken beats: `RUN.md` Story Point 0.
- **GM-only truth:** Ward is not lying. He believes the Rathaus is a real head. It is a **false head**. HQ circled it because it *was* the imperial post house. Ordinary extras only if they ask. Time-box: 8–10 minutes including a kit ask.
- **What changes over time:** if they linger, skip kit and extra chat and put them on the truck.
- **If violence begins:** do not play an attack on Ward. This is not a combat situation.
- **Relevant mechanics:** deferred.
- **Links:** Story Point 0; Story Point 0b.

- **First impression:**
  - **Climate:** Colder and darker than the classroom. Cloud. Wind through the airframe.
  - **See:** Four in one stick. No readable fields. Flak flashes under the flight — not a town they can name.
  - **Hear:** Engines. Wind. Bursts. Then a crew voice and a green light.
  - **Feel:** Thrill. Not a fight. The drop is already worse than the briefing.
- **Current situation:** green light anyway. Four in one stick.
- **Environment:** one transport. Dark. Flak flashes are spectacle.
- **People/forces present:** the four. Crew is color. Ward is not aboard.
- **What can be learned:** the drop is not the rehearsal; they are still together when they hit snow.
- **GM-only truth:** flak is en route, not this garrison. Dinkelsbühl stays asleep. `Athletics` here is flavor only — ignore Wounds, Fatigue, lost gear, scatter, and any raise that would improve the LZ.

- **If violence begins:** do not. No AA gunners to fight. No chute failure that benches a player.
- **Relevant mechanics:** `RUN.md` Story Point 0b at-hand rules. WW2 p.33 parachuting rebuilt to `Athletics` (SWADE p.29, p.118); flavor only. No d20 main-chute check. No Wounds, Fatigue, scatter, or a better LZ.
- **Links:** Story Point 0b; Story Point 1.

## Snow cover, unplanned face of the walls

- **Role in adventure:** first trouble on the objective map. They must orient, then choose a close. Player token: actual LZ — **after** they know where they are.
- **First impression:**
  - **Climate:** Heavy January night. Packed snow. Breath hanging. Wind off black water. Dark (−4).
  - **See:** Masonry wall close as a building. Ice and water on one side. Cover in ditch or tree line. A dark gate-arch; faint glow only if they look. Town asleep. They do not yet know which face this is.
  - **Hear:** Their own breath. Water against ice. No aircraft now. No alarm.
  - **Feel:** The town is calm. They are the intrusion. Cold. Short night. Not a firefight.
- **Current situation:** four together, east of the wall, south of Wörnitztor, off the Bahnhof. They do not know that when they hit snow. Night is short. No required sentry fight. A lamp or movement may show at Wörnitztor if they watch.
- **Environment:** winter dark, cold, snow, cover in ditch or tree line, water on one side, masonry on the other. Ice on the wall-walk. Height and ice are not an opening death gate. Wind off the water.
- **People/forces present:** the four. Distant thin watch at Wörnitztor. No station company. No contact NPC required. The town did not hear the aircraft as a local raid.
- **What can be learned:** they are on the wet face; Wörnitztor is the near gate; Segringer is the long circuit west; from a better angle the north wall may show an aerial on Grüner Turm; the Bahnhof is quiet. Orientation vectors: water on the briefing map vs water here; rehearsed west gate absent; Lang can name Wörnitztor (not the only vector).
- **GM-only truth:** they can reach town from here (Wörnitztor, a postern, or a short circuit). The long way only costs time. The live switchboard sits inside on the east walk they may use anyway. A failed Notice does not hide the water.
- **What changes over time:** cold, fatigue, less night. If they linger, Keller’s round may bring her to the east wall later, not at the first minute.
- **If violence begins:** at most the two at Wörnitztor, not a fortress sortie. Break-contact is available. Collateral is snow and noise.
- **Relevant mechanics:** deferred.
- **Links:** Story Point 1; Wörnitztor; streets; dummy site; any head.

- **First impression:**
  - **Climate:** Ice in the passage. Mill-race dark. Stove-smell if they get close.
  - **See:** A dark gate-arch. Gatehouse. A faint glow from a lamp or stove.
  - **Hear:** Stove. Water. Two tired voices if they are close.
  - **Feel:** Thin watch. They want the night to pass. Not a fortress.
- **Current situation:** two tired watchers, not the map’s ten. They want the night to pass. They will challenge a loud close. They will not teleport a company.
- **Environment:** gate passage, gatehouse, ice, short field of view along the east walk toward Altrathausplatz.
- **People/forces present:** two Extras (Volkssturm or convalescent). Keller is not here at start. Stein is not here at start.
- **What can be learned:** the town is thin; someone still walks the north wall; east lanes lead toward the old post / Altrathausplatz; the Rathaus is not on this face.
- **GM-only truth:** bypass (postern, circuit, climb) remains available. A botched gate is noise and a hunt seed, not a failed mission.
- **What changes over time:** if the fuse lights, this gate is an obvious plug. Dawn puts more eyes here.
- **If violence begins:** two people who want to live. Reinforcement is a shout, then minutes, then Keller or a runner — not a battalion. Civilians in nearby houses.
- **Relevant mechanics:** deferred.
- **Links:** LZ; streets; switchboard; extraction.

## The briefing building (dummy site) — Rathaus

- **Role in adventure:** the stale package. Optional visit. They came here for the **board**. Must pay if they come.
- **First impression:**
  - **Climate:** Same winter night. Less wind in the square. Cold coming down the street.
  - **See:** Civic pile on the market street. One lamp on the ground floor. No aerial on this roof. Tight older lanes. Blackout windows.
  - **Hear:** Almost nothing. No machine hum. No traffic. Maybe a stove behind a shutter.
  - **Feel:** A town that went to bed. An officers’ building, quiet, not a keep.
- **Current situation:** not the exchange. HQ’s circle is explainable (old post house) and still wrong. **Hofmann** is on the ground floor with a lamp, a stove, and old paper. **Stein** is in nearby quarters unless noise or a runner fetches him. **Brandt** is not here.
- **Environment:** offices, stair, winter blackout, a yard or side door. Not a dungeon. A dead wire stub or cut-over tag in a closet or cellar points **east**, not at the church.
- **People/forces present:** Hofmann for sure if they come in quiet hours. Stein if they are loud or late. A habit check at the door is possible (one person). Keller only if her round has been pulled here.
- **What can be learned:** this is not the hydra; traffic and the board moved; leftover note, tag, or Hofmann’s knowledge that *work* went to the old post / Altrathausplatz and that *books* live with the signals NCO toward **Nördlinger**; if they look north from the square, a stick of aerial on the north wall (loft) still live; Stein and Keller’s names and that they still treat this as *their* office. Hofmann names the north tower only if they ask.
- **GM-only truth:** wrecking only the Rathaus does not complete the mission. It can light the fuse. Give a **tell** if they treat it as a win: aerial still on the north wall, or a line still alive. Hofmann is one leftover, not the only one (wire, tag, habit patrol, empty board room).
- **What changes over time:** loud work here brings Stein downstairs and Keller off her round. Dawn fills the square with ordinary eyes.
- **If violence begins:** Hofmann first; Stein if woken (competent, not a super-villain). Street watch later. Civilians on the Weinmarkt. Reinforcement is local.
- **Relevant mechanics:** deferred.
- **Links:** Story Point 2; streets; Altrathausplatz; officers as fuse.

## Dinkelsbühl streets (lived-in town)

- **Role in adventure:** the space between heads. Civilian constraint lives here.
- **First impression:**
  - **Climate:** Same winter. Sound carries in the cold. First light only if dawn is on.
  - **See:** Tight lanes, snow in the ruts, church bulk, blackout or waking windows.
  - **Hear:** Shutters. Fuse: curfew shout. Else: a sleeping town.
  - **Feel:** Still thin, or a hunt — the night they earned.
- **Current situation:** most people are home. Signals people move with purpose only if the night is still theirs (board to loft, or Brandt to the board). Keller is on the north wall at start, not every street.
- **Environment:** ice, corners, wall-walk access at towers, almost no motors. Sound carries in the cold.
- **People/forces present:** civilians behind shutters; thin street watch only if already woken; a signals runner if the hydra is busy or wounded; Benoit only if they find that cellar.
- **What can be learned:** aerial on Grüner Turm from a north or high angle; hum or lamp at Altrathausplatz; empty feel at the Rathaus as an exchange; who holds the town (Stein / Keller); curfew habit; that the November companies on the map are not in these streets.
- **GM-only truth:** an alarm is a hunt in these streets first. Out-of-town help needs a living hydra or a long delay. Benoit can enter play here without being a required guide.
- **What changes over time:** fuse → pairs on lanes, a curfew shout, papers in motion. Dawn → more windows, more witnesses.
- **If violence begins:** short fields of fire, civilians in the line, thin opposition that can still kill a Seasoned PC once lethality is on. Standing constraint applies.
- **Relevant mechanics:** deferred.
- **Links:** all heads; fuse; Benoit.

## Switchboard (real exchange) — Altrathausplatz

- **Role in adventure:** one of three heads. **GM copy only.**
- **First impression:**
  - **Climate:** Heat after the street. Enclosed. A night lamp.
  - **See:** Working telephone room: cable, live board, two people, back door into lanes. Dwellings against the walls.
  - **Hear:** Clicks. Low winter traffic. A line that can still speak.
  - **Feel:** This room is awake. The square outside is not. Civilians next door.
- **Current situation:** two operators on the live board. Low winter traffic. They can still pass a landline scream if they believe the raid. Brandt is **not** in this square; he is on Nördlinger Straße.
- **Environment:** interior, cables, a back door to the lanes, dwellings against the walls. Fire here is a civilian problem.
- **People/forces present:** two signals Extras. A runner only after trouble. Stein / Keller only after the fuse. Benoit’s cellar is near, not in the exchange.
- **What can be learned:** the Rathaus was cut over or abandoned as an exchange; the loft is on the north wall (Grüner Turm); papers live with Brandt toward Nördlinger; they can still raise out of town if the loft lives. A smashed board with no papers and a live aerial is **one** head.
- **GM-only truth:** killing this is one hit. Loft + papers still complete the job. Coming through Wörnitztor can put them nearby without handing them the room.
- **What changes over time:** one other head dead → they try to patch or call. Alarm → they shout for Brandt or try the loft. Dawn → more traffic, more people.
- **If violence begins:** two operators who want the board to live or to get out. Reinforcement delayed and thin. Fire risks the square.
- **Relevant mechanics:** deferred.
- **Links:** Story Point 3; Brandt; loft; Wörnitztor.

## Aerial or radio loft — Grüner Turm

  - **Climate:** Wind and ice. Colder than the streets. Height.
  - **See:** Tall tower on the north wall. A stick of aerial if they have the angle. Tight stair. Ice on the wall-walk.
  - **Hear:** Wind. The set if they are close. Maybe a step on ice.

- **What can be learned:** they can still raise out of town; a runner path to the board or to Brandt; the church is not the aerial.
- **GM-only truth:** loft and feed are one head. Switchboard + papers still complete the job.
- **What changes over time:** board dead → they still try radio. Papers gone → less useful traffic. Fuse → Keller arrives with a pair, not a company.
- **If violence begins:** one watch plus height. Noise wakes the north face. Civilian risk is the tower and nearby houses, not a required church demolition.
- **Relevant mechanics:** deferred.

## Papers / Fw. Otto Brandt

- **Role in adventure:** one of three heads. A person and a satchel, not a unique lock. **GM copy only** for the starting pin.
- **First impression:**
  - **Climate:** Ordinary blackout street. Wood smoke. Less wind than the wall.
  - **See:** Commandeered room: stove, cot, a satchel or drawer. Street toward a southeast gate. Not a staff HQ.
  - **Hear:** The stove. A quiet lane. Maybe a sleeper a long way off.
  - **Feel:** Domestic. One NCO and a bag. Not a fortress.
- **Current situation:** Brandt is here at start with the live books and last traffic. He will move if the board or loft screams, if the Rathaus is hit loud, or if dawn comes and he walks to the exchange.
- **Environment:** one or two rooms, a street door, a way toward Wörnitztor or the board.
- **People/forces present:** Brandt. A runner only after trouble. Stein and Keller know he holds the books; they are not standing over him.
- **What can be learned:** codes and last traffic; the Rathaus is not the exchange; the board is on Altrathausplatz; the loft is the north tower; he can name Stein / Keller.
- **GM-only truth:** steal, burn, or scatter can kill this head. Brandt need not die. If the bag leaves, it is still findable (board, gate, stove, Stein’s office). Switchboard + loft still complete the job without him.
- **What changes over time:** alarm → bag toward a car, a gate, the board, or a stove. If he burns the books himself, that head can die without the PCs — they may not know. Do not hide a silent auto-success; give a sign (smoke, empty drawer, his flight).
- **If violence begins:** one competent NCO plus whoever arrives late. Capture and chase are available. Early capture of a *PC* here is recoverable the same night (Benoit or Voss).
- **Relevant mechanics:** deferred.
- **Links:** Story Point 5; switchboard; extraction.

## Gates, dawn, and the way out

- **Role in adventure:** closing pressure, and the other faces if they contour from the LZ. Not a second town.
- **First impression:**
  - **Climate:** Same winter. Ice. Dark (−4).
  - **See:** The walls they already know. Quieter faces than the east stove-gate.
  - **Hear:** Wind. Their own steps. A sleeper if someone is there.
  - **Feel:** The night they earned. Time is the cost, not a company.
- **Current situation at start:** **Wörnitztor** — 2 extras, stove. **Nördlinger Tor** — empty or 1 sleeper; Brandt’s street is inside. **Rothenburger Tor** — gate empty; Keller is on the wall-walk toward Grüner Turm, not in the passage. **Segringer Tor** — empty or 1 sleeper; long circuit from the east LZ. Postern / climb — no dedicated watch. Bahnhof — empty or 1 sleeper; not a fourth head; not the Altstadt door. Map numbers are false. Do not clone the east-gate pair. A circuit costs time, not a travel map.
- **Environment:** gates, posterns, wall-walk, snow fields. East water they already know. Ice. Dark (−4).
- **People/forces present:** whoever the fuse has woken — still a hollow town. Stein wants order. Keller wants the watch to work. A living hydra can call *later*, not instantly fill the map. A sleeper wants to be left alone.
- **What can be learned:** whether a call went out; which gate woke; whether the bag left town; south face feeds Nördlinger / Brandt; north face can show the aerial.
- **GM-only truth:** they can leave by more than one face. A long chase is a pacing cut. Other gates stay thinner even after the fuse; the gate they used becomes the obvious plug.
- **What changes over time:** first light; a successful out-of-town call only if two heads still live or landline still speaks.
- **If violence begins:** delaying action, not a required last stand. Civilian constraint still applies. Do not add a squad because they walked.
- **Relevant mechanics:** deferred.
