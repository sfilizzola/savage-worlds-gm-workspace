## Background (player)

Seasoned signals and language. She can smash gear as well as read it. The stale package offends her trade; she wants the real net. This station’s traffic and papers, not a civic facade.

**Hook:** wires, sets, and other people’s conversations.

**Stake:** kill the traffic, not the facade.

**The others:** German overlap with Keene (and Voss if he appears); Repair overlap with Krajewski; Healing d6 so Lang is not the only aid.
