Seasoned demolitions and field engineering. The briefed building may waste charges; improvisation is the job. Wreck what actually passes traffic, not masonry for its own sake.

**Hook:** the target pack may be wrong; the bag is still his.

**Stake:** kill the station, not the town.

**The others:** works with Vasseur on boards and sets; Keene calls the noise.
