# SAVAGE WORLDS — PLAYER CARD

Keep this beside your sheet. The GM has the book. You do not need it.

**You are a Wild Card.** You roll a extra **Wild Die (d6)** with every Trait. You can take **three Wounds**. You start with **3 Bennies**.

---

## How to roll

Roll the die on your sheet **and** a d6 (Wild Die). Keep the **higher**. Both can **Ace**: max on the die → roll again and add.

- **Success:** 4 or more after modifiers (Target Number 4 unless the GM says otherwise).
- **Raise:** every 4 over the Target Number. One raise is the extra effect (better info, +1d6 damage on an attack, and so on).
- **Opposed:** you still need a 4. Then they must match or beat you. Raises are counted from *their* total.
- **Unskilled:** no skill on the sheet → **d4−2** (and the Wild Die).
- **Critical Failure:** 1 on **both** Trait die and Wild Die. It fails badly. You **cannot** Benny this.

Typical modifiers: easy **+2**, hard **−2**, very hard **−4**. Wounds and Fatigue also subtract.

---

## Bennies (3)

Tokens. Spend them. Leftovers vanish at the end of the night.

- **Reroll a Trait.** Keep the best total. Critical Failure still stands.
- **Un-Shake instantly** (even off-turn).
- **Soak:** after damage, before Wounds land. Vigor; each success and raise soaks **one Wound** from that hit. Soak them all and you are not Shaken either. Once per attack; you may Benny the Vigor roll.
- **New Action Card** (in a fight, after cards are dealt).
- **Reroll damage.**
- **Nudge the story** — only if the GM allows it (a small item, a bit more talk, a missed detail).

A **Joker** on your Action Card: you act whenever you want this round, **+2** to Trait and damage, and **every player** gets a Benny.

---

## Your turn (about 6 seconds)

Cards: Ace down to 2. Ties: Spades, Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs. Joker goes when it wants.

On your card you may **move Pace 6″ (12 yards)** and take **one action**. Movement is not an action. Climb, crawl, swim, or rough ground: each inch costs **2″**.

**Run:** add a d6 to Pace this turn (does not Ace). **−2** to all your actions.

**Multi-action:** up to **three** actions. Two actions = **−2** to both. Three = **−4** to all three. Declare them before you roll.

**Free (no penalty):** a short sentence, drop something, go prone, stand (−2″ Pace), recover from Shaken/Stunned (once, start of turn), ready **two** familiar items.

**Hold:** wait. To interrupt someone, opposed **Athletics**. If you are Shaken or Stunned on Hold, you lose it.

## Hitting and hurting

| You try | Roll | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| Punch / knife / grab | **Fighting** | their **Parry** |
| Gun | **Shooting** | **4**, plus range |
| Grenade / thrown charge | **Athletics** | **4**, plus range |

**Range** (from the weapon on your sheet): Short —, Medium **−2**, Long **−4**. 1″ = 2 yards.

**Raise on the attack:** +1d6 damage (once, even with extra raises). Damage dice Ace. Melee damage is Strength + weapon — **no Wild Die**.

Then compare damage to **Toughness**:

- Less than Toughness → nothing that counts.
- Equal or higher → **Shaken**.
- Each **raise** on damage → a **Wound** (and Shaken).
- Already Shaken + another Shaken from **physical damage** → a Wound, still Shaken.

**Wild Cards (you):** three Wounds, then Incapacitated. Each Wound: **−1** Pace (min 1″) and **−1** to Trait rolls (max **−3**).

**Extras (most soldiers, clerks, watch):** **one Wound** and they are out.

**Shaken:** you may only move and take free actions (you can still Run). Start of your turn: **Spirit** as a free action — success and you act normally. Or spend a Benny anytime.

## Common actions

Say what you do. The GM names the Trait if it is not obvious.

| Do this | Trait | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| See, hear, catch a lie, find a clue | **Notice** | Darkness penalizes this |
| Sneak or hide | **Stealth** | vs Notice if they are looking. Sneak-up success: they are Vulnerable; raise: **The Drop**. In a fight, roll Stealth at the end of your move (free) |
| Climb, jump, swim, throw | **Athletics** | |
| Talk someone round | **Persuasion** | vs Spirit if they resist. **Limited by Language** if it is not your native tongue |
| Scare or bark an order | **Intimidation** | Same Language limit if you speak it |
| Insult / rattle | **Taunt** | vs Smarts. Usually a **Test** |
| Shoot | **Shooting** | |
| Fight up close | **Fighting** | |
| Fix, cut, or **set a charge** | **Repair** | Electronics gear is Limited by **Electronics** |
| Patch a Wound | **Healing** | 10 minutes per Wound level. Success heals 1, raise 2. Once per healer in the first hour |
| Help an ally’s roll | any fitting skill | **Support:** success +1, raise +2 (max +4 from all Support) |
| Put a foe off-balance | any fitting skill | **Test:** opposed by the skill’s linked attribute. Win: **Distracted** *or* **Vulnerable**. Raise: also **Shaken** |

**Limited by Language:** use the **lower** of the talking skill and that language. Your native tongue is never limited.

## Combat tricks (when it matters)

- **Aim** (whole turn, stay put): next turn’s first shot ignores 4 points of range / cover / called shot — *or* +2 to hit.
- **Defend** (whole turn, no extra actions, no Run): **+4 Parry** until your next turn.
- **Wild Attack** (melee): **+2** Fighting and damage; you are **Vulnerable** until the end of your *next* turn.
- **Called Shot:** limb **−2**; head or vitals **−4** and **+4** damage. Open-faced helmet, shot to the **face:** **−5** and the helmet does not count.
- **Cover:** −2 / −4 / −6 / −8 (quarter / half / three-quarters / barely visible). Prone vs ranged at 3″+ is **−4** (does not stack with cover).
- **The Drop** (they are bound or truly unaware): **+4** attack and damage for one action.
- **Auto (RoF 3, Sten):** more than one shot in one action → **Recoil −2**. You may fire fewer shots. Reloading a magazine is an **action**.
- **Withdraw** from someone adjacent: they get a free attack. **Extraction** (on the sheet) skips one of them.
- **Gang up:** each extra adjacent attacker adds +1 Fighting (max +4).

**Distracted:** −2 to your Trait rolls until the end of your next turn.  
**Vulnerable:** others add +2 to actions against you until then.

## This night

- Default light is **Dark (−4)** on attacks, Notice, and similar. You cannot see a target past **10″ (20 yards)** without a lamp or a break in the dark. Dim (−2) only if the GM says the moon or a lamp is enough. Pitch (−6) is a sealed unlit room.
- **Helmet** does **not** raise Toughness. It is **Armor +2** only against Called Shots to the **head**.
- Rank titles are speech. They do **not** give extra Bennies.
- Team satchels, TNT, cutters, civilian coats: **anyone** may use them. Repair is the demolitions skill.
- Edges, languages, and weapons: **your sheet**. If an Edge would change a roll, say so before you throw the dice.

When you are stuck: say what your soldier does. Rolling comes after.
