Savage Worlds — Player card

Keep beside your sheet

Same card for everyone. Your numbers, Edges, and weapons stay on the character sheet. The GM has the book.

You are a Wild Card. Roll your Trait die and a Wild Die (d6); keep the higher. You can take three Wounds. You start with 3 Bennies.

How to roll

Keep the higher of Trait die and Wild Die. Both can Ace (max on the die → roll again and add).

  • Success: 4+ after modifiers (TN 4 unless told otherwise).
  • Raise: every 4 over the TN. One raise is the extra effect (better detail, +1d6 damage on an attack).
  • Opposed: you still need a 4. They must match or beat you. Raises count from their total.
  • Unskilled: no skill → d4−2 (+ Wild Die).
  • Critical Failure: 1 on both dice. Fails badly. You cannot Benny it.

Easy +2, hard −2, very hard −4. Wounds and Fatigue also subtract.

Bennies (3)

Spend them. Unused tokens vanish at the end of the night.

  • Reroll a Trait — keep the best total (not a Critical Failure).
  • Un-Shake instantly — even off-turn.
  • Soak — after damage, before Wounds. Vigor; each success and raise soaks one Wound from that hit. Soak them all and you are not Shaken. Once per attack; you may Benny the Vigor roll.
  • New Action Card in a fight (after the deal).
  • Reroll damage.
  • Nudge the story — only if the GM allows (a small item, a bit more talk).

Joker on your card: act whenever you want this round, +2 Trait and damage, and every player gets a Benny.

Your turn (~6 seconds)

Cards: Ace down to 2. Ties: Spades, Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs.

Move Pace 6″ (12 yards) and take one action. Movement is not an action. Climb, crawl, swim, or rough ground: 1″ costs 2″.

  • Run: +d6 Pace (does not Ace), −2 to all actions.
  • Multi-action: up to three. Two = −2 to both. Three = −4 to all. Declare before you roll.
  • Free: a short sentence, drop an item, prone, stand (−2″ Pace), recover Shaken/Stunned once at the start of your turn, ready two familiar items.
  • Hold: wait. Interrupt with opposed Athletics. Shaken or Stunned on Hold loses it.

Range: Short —, Medium −2, Long −4. 1″ = 2 yards.

Raise on the attack: +1d6 damage (once). Damage dice Ace. Melee is Strength + weapon — no Wild Die.

Damage vs Toughness:

  • Below Toughness → no effect that counts.
  • Equal or higher → Shaken.
  • Each raise on damage → a Wound (and Shaken).
  • Already Shaken + another Shaken from physical damage → a Wound, still Shaken.

You: three Wounds, then Incapacitated. Each Wound: −1 Pace (min 1″) and −1 Trait (max −3).

Extras (most watch, clerks, soldiers): one Wound and they are out.

Shaken: only move and free actions (you may still Run). Start of turn: Spirit as a free action — success and you act. Or spend a Benny anytime.

Combat tricks

  • Aim (whole turn, stay put): next first shot ignores 4 points of range/cover/called shot — or +2 to hit.
  • Defend (whole turn, no extra actions, no Run): +4 Parry until your next turn.
  • Wild Attack (melee): +2 Fighting and damage; you are Vulnerable until the end of your next turn.
  • Called Shot: limb −2; head/vitals −4 and +4 damage. Face of an open helmet: −5, helmet does not count.
  • Cover: −2 / −4 / −6 / −8. Prone vs ranged at 3″+ is −4 (does not stack with cover).
  • The Drop (bound or truly unaware): +4 attack and damage, one action.
  • Auto (RoF 3): more than one shot in one action → Recoil −2. You may fire fewer. Reload a magazine = an action.
  • Withdraw: adjacent foes get a free attack. Extraction (if on your sheet) skips one.
  • Gang up: +1 Fighting per extra adjacent attacker (max +4).

Distracted: −2 Trait until the end of your next turn. Vulnerable: others +2 against you until then.

This night

  • Default light is Dark (−4) on attacks, Notice, and similar. No target visible past 10″ (20 yards) without a lamp or a break in the dark.
  • Helmet does not raise Toughness. Armor +2 only vs Called Shots to the head.
  • Rank titles are speech — no extra Bennies.
  • Team satchels, TNT, cutters, civilian coats: anyone may use them. Repair is demolitions.
  • Edges live on your sheet. If one would change a roll, say so before the dice.
