# HINTERLAND — GM CARD 1/2

Tonight. Not for players. `RUN.md` still runs the situations.

**Any two real heads and the hydra dies.** Board · loft · books. The Rathaus is a **false head** — cutting it does not count. One head off = wounded; it can still scream. Hollow town. No occult. Gender is not a table question.

## Tracker

- ☐ Board (Altrathausplatz)
- ☐ Loft (Grüner Turm)
- ☐ Books (Brandt / satchel)
- ☐ Fuse (Stein / Keller)
- ☐ Scream attempted
- ☐ Dawn / civilian harm

Two of the three head boxes = job done. Rathaus does not get a box.

## What kills a head

- **Board:** smash, cut cables, kill power, or fire. Repair / Electronics to wreck the set. Smash still works if Repair fails. Fire here is a civilian problem.
- **Loft:** wreck the set, or cut the feed at the base or wall-walk. Climb is not the only method. Slip = noise / maybe a Wound, not a bench.
- **Books:** steal, burn, or scatter the satchel. Brandt need not die. If the bag leaves, it is still findable (board, gate, stove, Stein’s office).

Hitting one and leaving is not the job. Restate the hydra rule if they stop.

## Finding (GM copy)

They already know the *types*. Locations stay hidden until found. Need two channels; three is better. Hofmann is never the only vector.

| Head | How they find the place |
|---|---|
| Board | Leftover / Hofmann: east, old post. Dead wire east. Lamp or hum on Altrathausplatz; east walk from Wörnitztor. |
| Loft | Skyline from a north or high angle. Feed at the base. Keller’s stretch is a sighting, not the key. |
| Books | Leftover: Feldwebel toward Nördlinger. Operators name Brandt. Stein / Keller know who holds the bag. Satchel can move. |

Skip the Rathaus: streets still pay (aerial, hum/lamp). Dummy wreck is not a win — give a tell (aerial still up, or a line still alive).

## Night, light, cold

- Default **Dark (−4)** on attacks, Notice, and similar. No target visible past **10″ (20 yd)** without a lamp or a break. SWADE p.102.
- 6–7 Jan, Last Quarter (moon ~midnight). First half stays Dark. After moonrise, a clear snow sky or a lamp/stove can be **Dim (−2)**.
- **Pitch (−6)** only in an unlit cellar or sealed room.
- Jump Athletics is flavor only: no Wounds, Fatigue, lost gear, scatter, or a better LZ. They land together.
- No hidden Fatigue at the LZ. If they linger exposed: Hazards p.125.
- Ice / height / climb: Athletics. Rough ground: 1″ costs 2″.
- Lang’s Woodsman +2 Stealth: fields / Wörnitz tree line only — not streets, indoors, or the wall-walk (p.50).

## Gates (hollow)

Closing from the east LZ is a short correction, not a travel map. Play the gate they arrive at. Do not stack a pair at every door.

|---|---|

| Nördlinger SE | Empty or 1 sleeper (same Extra, maybe no rifle). Brandt’s street inside. |
| Rothenburger N | Gate empty. Keller on the wall-walk toward Grüner Turm. |
| Segringer W | Empty or 1 sleeper. Long circuit; night shortens. Rathaus just inside. |

| Bahnhof | Not a door into the Altstadt. Not a fourth head. |

Map names: Kohlenburger → Rothenburger (N). Sägringer → Segringer (W). If the fuse is lit, the gate they used becomes an obvious plug. Other faces stay thinner. Do not add a squad.

## Clocks and scream

| Trigger | What changes |
|---|---|
| Time on the dummy | Night shortens. Hydra still up. |
| One head off | Wounded. Can still scream. |
| Two heads off | Hydra dead. Hunt may still be live. |
| Officers lit | Local hunt / curfew. Not a battalion. |
| Dawn | More eyes. Extraction expensive. |

**Scream** = the hydra calling: landline from a live board, radio from a live loft, or papers still useful enough to pass traffic. Out-of-town help needs a *living path and time*. En-route flak does not wake this town. One Stealth fail is not an army.

## Fuse hunt

- Start with **one pair** of Volkssturm extras (same block as Wörnitztor). Add Stein and/or Keller when the fiction puts them there.
- **Move** pins. Do not empty other heads. Do not add Experienced infantry, Dodge, Rock and Roll!, or MG42.
- Stein in Command Range (5″ / 10 yd): extras +1 Toughness (Hold the Line!). They are still Extras. Command helps them recover from Shaken / Stunned. Neither officer has Natural Leader — Leadership does not buff player Wild Cards. Keene’s Command is the same: allied Extras only.
- After they have acted in town, lethality is on. Telegraph it. Four Seasoned vs two extras is still favored; vs two extras + a Wild Card officer it is a real fight.

## Failure still plays

- Ugly jump → description only. Failed Notice at the LZ → water is still in front of them.
- Dummy wreck → leftover still points onward.
- Botched loft climb → noise, not a bench. Feed at the base remains.
- Brandt flees → bag still findable, or skip this head (board + loft).
- Hofmann freezes → wire stub, tag, empty board room.
- One humming room and they stop → restate: one head is not the job.

**Do not import:** SS / Fanatic / HJ · Experienced / Elite Edges (Dodge, Rock and Roll!, Élan) · Sanity · Rank extra Benny / +2 Toughness on NPCs · Demo Man, Medic!, Jump Qualified · any Chapter 10 horror. Rank titles are speech.

# HINTERLAND — GM CARD 2/2

Default TN 4. Extras: no Wild Die; one Wound and they are out (p.95). Wild Card NPCs: Wild Die, 3 Wounds, **2 Bennies** when they appear (p.89). GM pool: **1 per player (4)** — spend on any NPC (reroll, un-Shake, Soak for Wild Cards).

## Combat, this night

- **NPC shooting is bad in the dark.** Dark −4 on their Shooting too. Kar **Snapfire −2** if the shooter moved (p.66). Auto more than one shot in one action: **Recoil −2** (p.105) — nobody here has Rock and Roll!, including the PCs. Noise still lights the fuse even when shots miss.
- **Helmet:** Armor +2 vs Called Shots to the head only. Does not raise Toughness. No 50% save. Face of an open helmet: −5, bypasses the helmet. (pp. 98–99; RUL-2026-08-29-01)
- Called Shot: limb −2; head/vitals −4 and +4 damage.
- Cover −2 / −4 / −6 / −8. Prone vs ranged at 3″+ is −4 (does not stack with cover).
- **The Drop** (bound or truly unaware): +4 attack and damage, one action. Sneak-up success: Vulnerable; raise: The Drop.
- Withdraw: adjacent foes get a free attack. Extraction skips one (Keene, Lang, Voss).
- Shaken: Spirit as a free action at start of turn, or a Benny. Combat Reflexes: +2 to that Spirit (Krajewski, Vasseur, Keller, Voss).
- Soak (Wild Cards): Benny + Vigor after damage, before Wounds. Each success/raise soaks one Wound from that hit.
- Lang Parry **2** (no Fighting). Do not stand her in melee.
- Chase p.113 only if you actually run one; otherwise a short street situation. Do not force a Dramatic Task.

## Team and Edges that fire

Team kit, anyone: 2× small satchel, 4× ½-lb TNT, cutters, 4× civilian coats and papers. Anyone may set a charge. **Repair** is demolitions (p.34). Repair of electronic gear is **Limited by Electronics** — use the lower die (p.34). Krajewski Repair d8 / Electronics d6. Vasseur both d8. Fire, smash, and cut still work if Repair fails.

- **Alertness +2 Notice** — Keene, Vasseur, Lang.
- **Mr. Fix It +2 Repair** — Krajewski, Vasseur, Voss.
- **Healer +2 Healing** — Lang. Healing: 10 min per Wound level; success 1, raise 2; once per healer in the first hour. Vasseur Healing d6 so Lang is not the only aid.
- **McGyver** — Krajewski; improvised tools, not a unique key.
- **Soldier** — Krajewski; Strength one die higher for load / Min Str (p.67).
- **Linguist** — Vasseur. Verbal skills Limited by Language (p.32): lower of the talking skill and German. Native tongue never limited. Keene German d6; Vasseur d6; Lang d4; Voss d6.
- **Thief** — Voss (re-entry). Talk is never required.

| Do | Trait |
|---|---|

| Sneak the gate / streets | Stealth vs their Notice (gate d4; Keller d8) |
| Orient / postern / ice close | Survival (Woodsman +2 in the tree line only) |
| Talk Hofmann, watch, Brandt | Persuasion vs Spirit if they resist; Limited by German |
| Scare a sleeper | Intimidation (same Language limit) |
| Climb, throw, slip on ice | Athletics |
| Cut, charge, smash a set | Repair (electronics Limited by Electronics) |
| Read whether a line can still scream | Electronics |
| Help an ally | Support: success +1, raise +2 (max +4) |
| Unbalance a foe | Test: win Distracted or Vulnerable; raise also Shaken |

Information rolls: always state fail / success / raise. A failed Notice, Persuasion, or Repair never stops the night — it costs time, noise, or a tell.

## PC snapshot

- **Keene** — Parry 5. Shooting d8, Athletics d8, Notice d8. Command (Extras only). Extraction. Holds the four; do not spend them on the dummy.
- **Krajewski** — Parry 5. Repair d8, Shooting d8, Str d8, Spirit d4. Counts charges. Driven: wreck traffic, not masonry.
- **Vasseur** — Parry 5. Electronics d8, Repair d8, Sten (Auto). Curious / Cautious. Smash works.
- **Lang** — Parry 2. Stealth d8, Healing d8, Survival d8, Spirit d8. Names every gate. Not melee.
- **Voss** (reserve) — Parry 5. Stealth d8, Repair d8, Thievery d6. Missed this lift; after the first hour, or a fifth from the start.

All Wild Cards, Pace 6, Toughness 5, 3 Bennies. If a player is removed: prefer Voss; else promote Benoit (Wild Die + 3 Bennies, p.87). Do not leave a player in the hall. Captured PC is a same-night stake.

## Briefing marks / climax

Player map at SP0: planned DZ (west / Segringer) + Rathaus only. Actual east LZ marked after they orient. Never mark live heads on Handout A.

**Climax:** second head + live fuse. Opposition = remaining watch, officer if lit, one hunt pair. Secondary objective: finish the head or stop the scream *and* get the four off the board. Civilians in the line are a cost, not a hidden auto-fail. If time is short: one location, fuse on the table, then “you are out, at this cost.” Long vehicle chase is the cut.

End: 1 Advance only if these characters continue (p.54). Otherwise the night is the ending.
