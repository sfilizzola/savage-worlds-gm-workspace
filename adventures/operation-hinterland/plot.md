# Plot Model

This is not a scene script. Predetermined truths live in `ADVENTURE.md`. This file tracks the throughline, reachable story points, and what failure changes.

## Table vocabulary

Shorthand for this adventure. Compile into `RUN.md`. Not player-facing unless the GM chooses to say it.

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **Hydra** | The whole rear signals station. Not one building. |
| **Head** | One killable part. There are three: **board** (Altrathausplatz), **loft** (Grüner Turm), **books** (Brandt’s satchel). Cut **any two** and the hydra dies. |
| **False head** | The Rathaus. Ward pins it. Cutting it does **not** count. |
| **Wounded hydra** | One real head off, two still live. It can still **scream**. |
| **Scream** | The hydra calling for help: landline from the board, radio from the loft, or papers still useful enough to pass traffic. |
| **Dummy site** | Same as false head: the Rathaus. Empty as an exchange. Officers may still keep an office there. |
| **Fuse** | Stein and/or Keller starting a local hunt or curfew. Not a battalion arriving. |

## Main throughline

- GM's desired destination: the Dinkelsbühl hydra cannot pass useful traffic or papers. The four can still be on the board when that is true or false.
- What must remain flexible: order of sites, methods (stealth, talk, force, wrecking, fire, theft, cutting lines), whether they visit the dummy building, how they leave.
- What failure changes: time, noise, which part of the net still lives, whether officers have lit the fuse, extraction cost, civilian harm. Failure does not mean "you never find a radio."

## Story Point 0 - Briefing in Pont-à-Mousson

- **Purpose:** put HQ’s picture of the town in the players’ hands, let the four meet, and mark only what the briefing is allowed to know.
- **Reachable when:** first thing at the table.
- **Situation:** commandeered schoolroom, Allied Lorraine, behind the fighting. **Capt. Ellis Ward** (U.S., intel/ops) believes the pack. Hand the player copy of BG-OH-001. Mark **planned DZ** (west / Segringer Tor) and the **Rathaus** as the named target. Do **not** mark the actual east LZ. Optional: they may ask around the base for ordinary winter/infantry extras. Full steps: `RUN.md` Story Point 0 and `maps/README.md`.
- **Essential information:** Operation Hinterland; night of 6–7 January; Nordwind is the clock, Dinkelsbühl is the target (signals station still talking toward Alsace); November sheet: walls, east water, four gates; west DZ / Segringer Tor; thin rear garrison, not a fortress; the station is a hydra; three **types** (board / aerial / books); cut two and it dies (one is not enough); HQ pinned the Rathaus as the **exchange**; find the aerial and the books on the ground; Stein and Keller named as responsible, not the objective; extraction after two heads are off. No second mission. Full spoken beats: `RUN.md` Story Point 0.
- **Independent vectors:** Ward’s speech (hydra rule is **handed here**, not later); the marks; the map’s water drawn on the east (they will need this later).
- **Escalation:** none. Do not start a fight. If the room talks too long, skip the kit ask and extra chat and put them on the truck.
- **Failure consequences:** if you skip the marks, they have no shared picture and the stale-package twist has no object. Stop and mark. A refused kit ask does not change the mission.
- **Links forward:** Story Point 0b.
- **Optional/essential:** essential. Kit ask is optional.

## Story Point 0b - The jump

- **Purpose:** thrill and the cause of the missed DZ. Spend little clock. No mechanical teeth.
- **Reachable when:** the briefing is done (Story Point 0).
- **Situation:** one transport, weather worse than briefed, west fields not readable, flak **en route** (not Dinkelsbühl’s). Each PC may roll `Athletics` once for chute flavor. WW2 parachuting (p.33) is rebuilt to Athletics in `RUN.md` SP0b as a second at-hand card — narrate only; outcome and LZ do not change. They land **together**.
- **Essential information:** the drop did not go as rehearsed; they are still a stick of four.
- **Independent vectors:** the aircraft; the weather; the dark DZ. No Notice gate to “be allowed to land.”
- **Escalation:** none that changes Dinkelsbühl. The town stays asleep. Do not apply Wounds, Fatigue, lost gear, scatter, or a better LZ.
- **Failure consequences:** ugly description only. Same snow.
- **Links forward:** Story Point 1.
- **Optional/essential:** essential as the insertion; the rolls are optional flavor.

## Story Point 1 - Wrong face of the walls

- **Purpose:** they discover they are on the unplanned east face and choose a close. Spend little clock.
- **Reachable when:** they have jumped (Story Point 0b).
- **Situation:** together in cover, snow, short night, **east / Wörnitz face**, south of Wörnitztor, off the Bahnhof. They do **not** know that when they hit snow. The rehearsed close is **Segringer Tor** (west). Orient from the briefing map, then close. Other gates are **thinner** than Wörnitztor (empty or one sleeper). A circuit costs time, not a travel map. Keller is on the north wall-walk, not in Rothenburger Tor. Full gate card: `RUN.md` Gates and the circuit.
- **Essential information:** they can reach the town tonight from here; they are on the wet / east face, not the rehearsed west; the long way costs time they may want for the hydra; other entrances are not copies of the east-gate pair.
- **Independent vectors:** water on the map vs water here; the rehearsed west gate is not in front of them; Lang can name Wörnitztor (PC hook, not the only vector); a road, ditch, or postern on this face; a distant sound or light from inside (not one NPC guide).
- **Escalation:** if they take the long way, night shortens. If they freeze, cold and dawn do the work.
- **Failure consequences:** late arrival, colder, more tired. They still reach a face of the town. A failed Notice does not hide the water.
- **Links forward:** dummy building, streets, any part of the net, officer fuse if they make a loud approach.
- **Optional/essential:** essential as the opening situation on the objective map; the correction itself is light.

## Story Point 2 - The briefing building is wrong

- **Purpose:** the real improvisation. The **Rathaus** on BG-OH-001 is empty as an exchange, moved, or was never the exchange.
- **Reachable when:** they check the briefed site, overhear, watch wire, or ask; also reachable if they never go there and read the town instead.
- **Situation:** the dummy site can still pay — leftover sign, a patrol habit, a clerk, a wire run toward the real switchboard or loft.
- **Essential information:** the Rathaus is a **false head**; the three real heads are board, loft, books.
- **Independent vectors:** the dummy site itself; street observation (aerial, hum, sentries); civilians or a clerk; the officers' routines; cut or followed wire.
- **Escalation:** time spent here is time the watch can move papers or wake a stronger eye. If they wreck only this building, that does not count as a head.
- **Failure consequences:** they lose time or make noise at the wrong door. The three real heads remain findable.
- **Links forward:** any of the three real heads; officer attention if they were loud.
- **Optional/essential:** essential as a truth; visiting the dummy building is optional.

## Story Point 3 - Switchboard

- **Purpose:** one real head (the board). Killing it is one of the two hits needed.
- **Reachable when:** they have any vector to the real exchange (wire, watch pattern, civilian, leftover from the dummy site, following an operator).
- **Situation:** in town, watched by the signals detachment more than by street garrison. Civilians live nearby.
- **Essential information:** this is the switchboard; it can still pass landline traffic if the loft and papers live; wrecking it is not the only method (power, lines, operators, fire, theft of the board's usefulness).
- **Independent vectors:** cut-over / wire from the Rathaus east; lamp or hum on Altrathausplatz; Wörnitztor east walk; operator or leftover talk. Not “enter Wörnitztor or the night stops.”
- **Escalation:** if left intact while another part dies, operators can patch or scream. If hit first and loudly, officers start the local hunt.
- **Failure consequences:** failed entry, alarm, or a botched wreck leaves the board wounded or still up. Other heads remain.
- **Links forward:** loft, papers, extraction, fuse.
- **Optional/essential:** essential as a target; not a mandatory first stop.

## Story Point 4 - Aerial or radio loft

- **Purpose:** second head. **Grüner Turm**, north wall. The aerial is visible in principle.
- **Reachable when:** they see the aerial, follow feed line, watch a runner, or get testimony.
- **Situation:** more exposed than the switchboard; winter wind and height; a small watch, not a fortress roof.
- **Essential information:** this is the radio loft / aerial; killing it guts air traffic; landline may still live.
- **Independent vectors:** skyline; wire run; sentry pattern; someone who hates the noise or the light.
- **Escalation:** a living loft can call out of town once the watch believes the raid is real.
- **Failure consequences:** fall, noise, or a missed charge. Climbing is not the only method (feed line at the base, fire from below, cutting stays).
- **Links forward:** switchboard, papers, extraction, fuse.
- **Optional/essential:** essential as a target; not a mandatory first stop.

## Story Point 5 - Papers and the signals NCO

- **Purpose:** third head. Codebooks and traffic. The NCO is a person, not a locked box that only one PC can open.
- **Reachable when:** they identify the NCO, the billets, a courier, or the satchel's route.
- **Situation:** papers move. Default start: **Brandt’s billet on Nördlinger Straße**, not on Altrathausplatz and not in the Rathaus. The bag may be on him, in the room, or in motion.
- **Essential information:** where the live papers are tonight; burning, stealing, or scattering them counts; the NCO need not die.
- **Independent vectors:** the NCO's habits; a clerk; a satchel; the dummy site leftover; an officer who knows who holds the books.
- **Escalation:** if the raid is loud, papers try to leave town or burn on German terms. If ignored, they keep the hydra useful even after a smashed set.
- **Failure consequences:** NCO escapes with the bag, or the bag is destroyed where the PCs cannot confirm. Other heads remain. The NCO is not the only path to the main plot.
- **Links forward:** remaining heads, fuse, extraction.
- **Optional/essential:** essential as a target; capturing the NCO is optional.

## Story Point 6 - Fuse and extraction

- **Purpose:** end the night under the cost they earned.
- **Reachable when:** after noise, after dawn, after a wounded hydra screams, or after they choose to leave.
- **Situation:** hollow town becoming a hunt or a waking street. Named officers act as the fuse, not as a battalion arriving by teleport.
- **Essential information:** whether the hydra can still call; which way out is still open; what the standing civilian constraint has already cost.
- **Independent vectors:** their own route; a gate or wall they already used; stolen transport; hiding until another hour — none of these is the only exit.
- **Escalation:** dawn; a successful out-of-town call if two heads still live or landline still speaks; hostage or curfew if officers are provoked.
- **Failure consequences:** costly exit, capture of gear, a PC down after they have already played. The mission result (hydra dead or not) is already decided by then or still decidable in flight.
- **Links forward:** end states.
- **Optional/essential:** essential as the closing pressure; a long chase is a likely cut.

## Possible end states

- Full success: any two heads dead; papers useless if they were one of the two; officers not yet able to call real help; team leaves in the dark.
- Costly success: hydra dead; dawn, wounded, civilian harm, or a loud hunt on the way out.
- Partial success: only one head off; hydra wounded; a call may go out; extraction expensive; southern-front help delayed, not stopped.
- Main-objective failure: two heads still live, or papers plus one live path still pass traffic. They may still escape. The window of German coordination holds.
- Unexpected outcome: papers stolen instead of burned; an officer captured; the town bloodied; the dummy site blamed and the real net quiet for a night for the wrong reason. Record what actually happened; do not rewrite unused prep as history.
