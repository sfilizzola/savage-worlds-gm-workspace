# Characters

Pregenerated only. Entry **Seasoned** (4 Advances). SWADE creation; Weird War II for period kit names and weapon tables. Old WW2 Edges (Rank, Demo Man, Medic!) are **not** used.

## Party assumptions

- Rank: Seasoned
- Player count: 4
- Allowed character sources: SWADE Fifth Printing (2023); Weird War II setting material rebuilt against SWADE; no other modules
- Required competencies without requiring one specific skill/Edge: the party as a whole can close quietly, wreck a set or a loft, deal with German speech, and keep a wounded teammate in play. Each competency exists on at least two PCs.
- Shared reason to engage: they accepted the same deep raid; they brief together in Pont-à-Mousson and jump the same stick; turning around in this snow is not a free exit from the premise.
- Real-life call letters (player invitations, not table canon): `handouts/`.

## Early removal mitigation

- Opening risks that could hard-remove a PC: a lethal drop or scatter (not used — jump is flavor only); a climb or first sentry as a single kill roll; splitting on the wrong face; capture at the dummy building.
- Telegraphing or recovery built into those risks: jump cannot Wound, Fatigue, strip gear, or split them; they land together in cover; no required fight; orientation is not a single Notice gate; dummy building is optional and not a fortress; first violence has an out (break contact, civilians, thin watch).
- Reserve pregen or allied character: **Cpl. Pieter Voss** (build with the four). Allied Extra: **Pvt. Louis Benoit**, hidden in town (cellar or Altrathausplatz outbuilding). Neither is the only intel source.
- Fictional entry point for replacement: Voss missed this lift and finds the team after the first hour; or Benoit is already in a cellar / loft / dummy-site office and can be handed to the player.
- Procedure for capture/separation without sidelining the player: captured PC becomes a mobile stake the others can reach the same night, or the player takes the reserve immediately. Do not leave a player in the hall.

## Later lethality

After the team has acted on the town and the danger is visible (shots, a hunt, a loft fight, papers on the run), wounds and death are on the table. Communicate it through the fuse: officers moving, dawn, a living hydra trying to call. This is not invisible immunity in the opening, and not a coin-flip corpse before they have played.
