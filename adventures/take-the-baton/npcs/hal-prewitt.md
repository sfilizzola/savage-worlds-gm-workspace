# Hal Prewitt

Adventure-local. Preparation only until played.

- **Role:** Colston's head coach in 2001, now retired and living alone at the edge of town. Carries 25 years of suppressed, half-acknowledged guilt.
- **What he knows:** he has seen Jesse in the years since 2001 and told himself it was grief. He privately kept clippings on Wesley Corbin (1966) and Denise Kowalski (1983) after noticing the resemblance to Jesse's case, but has never told another living soul, including his late wife. He does not know what the entity is or how it works — only that "something about the win" has always felt wrong to him.
- **What he doesn't know / must not say:** the entity's true nature, the shaft fence's exact mechanism, or anything about tonight's specific plan. He is not a font of complete answers — only a second source for the same fragmentary truths available elsewhere.
- **Wants:** to be left alone with his guilt, and, underneath that, to finally say the thing out loud to someone who already half-believes him.
- **Fears:** being thought senile or grief-broken; that saying it out loud makes it real and his fault.
- **Relationship to the PCs:** was their coach; closest personally to Toby, who he still sees around town.
- **Voice:** clipped and deflecting at first — coach's cadence, giving instructions even when there's no play to call. Breaks into something quieter and more halting if truly reached.
- **Key lines:** "Jesse drowned. That's what happened. That's all that happened." / (if he breaks) "I used to see him. Out past the fence line. Told myself it was grief. Told myself that for twenty-five years." / "Don't let that girl run anchor. I know how that sounds."
- **Stats:** not a combat NPC. No stat block.
- **GM Note:** see `secrets.md`, Secrets 1 and 3, and `plot.md` Story Point 4. If PCs push too hard too fast, he can shut the door for the day — but remains reachable again later that evening, more shaken, especially if approached by Toby or shown evidence from the archives.
