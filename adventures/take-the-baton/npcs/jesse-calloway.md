# Jesse Calloway

Adventure-local. Preparation only until played.

- **Role:** the PCs' fifth teammate, anchor leg, 2001. Hollowed by the entity under Colston's field weeks after that year's win; has lingered near the field and Miller's Hole ever since.
- **What he knows:** almost nothing in any way he could report. What's left of him recognizes the four PCs, recognizes danger to Nora, and recognizes the shaft fence as a place that matters. He does not know what he is, cannot explain the entity, and cannot narrate the past 25 years.
- **What he doesn't know / must not say:** anything expository. He never explains the mechanism, the 1966/1983 pattern, or what will happen to Nora in game terms. He is not a source of exposition under any circumstance.
- **Wants:** to protect Nora, dimly and urgently, the way a person underwater still reaches for the surface.
- **Fears:** nothing articulable; if he has any coherent feeling left, it is closer to grief than fear.
- **Relationship to the PCs:** loved by all four, differently — see `characters.md`. He reacts most to Toby and Renee, the two he was closest to.
- **Voice:** almost none. He does not hold conversation. At most, one or two fragments per scene: her name, a single warning word, a wordless gesture. Never write him a monologue or a full sentence of exposition.
- **Key lines (use sparingly, no more than one or two per scene):** "Nora—" / "Don't." / silence, and a slow turn of the head toward whoever's looking.
- **Stats:** not a combat NPC. No stat block; he is never an opponent and never fights.
- **GM Note:** full truth in `secrets.md`, Secret 1. His stillness and unaged appearance are the only "supernatural" signal he gives off directly — no glowing eyes, no monstrous reveal. The horror is that he is exactly as he was, and everyone else has stopped seeing him.
