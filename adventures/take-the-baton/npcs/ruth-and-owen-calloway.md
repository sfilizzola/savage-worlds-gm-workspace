# Ruth and Owen Calloway

Adventure-local. Preparation only until played. Optional scene; not required for any essential information.

- **Role:** Jesse's parents, still living in Colston.
- **What they know:** they sincerely believe Jesse drowned in 2001. They have never seen him since, though both admit, if asked gently, to an inexplicable pull toward the field on certain evenings they've never examined too closely.
- **What they don't know / must not say:** anything about the entity, the pattern, or Jesse's continued presence. They are not withholding — they simply were never shown otherwise.
- **Wants:** to be at the assembly and the field tonight, honoring their son's memory alongside his old team.
- **Fears:** being forgotten now that 25 years have passed; polite dread of being asked to relive that year in detail.
- **Relationship to the PCs:** warm, a little fragile — grateful the team came back, quick to change the subject away from the details of that spring.
- **Voice:** careful, practiced grief — the version of the story they've told so many times it comes out smooth.
- **Key lines:** Ruth: "It means so much that you all came back for him." Owen, if pressed gently: "I still walk out to the field some evenings. I couldn't tell you why."
- **Stats:** not a combat NPC. No stat block.
- **GM Note:** do not have either of them confirm or deny anything about Jesse's true state — they genuinely don't know. Their unexamined pull toward the field is color, not a clue to be mechanically rolled for.
