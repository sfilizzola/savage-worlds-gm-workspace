# Arlene Combs

Adventure-local. Preparation only until played.

- **Role:** Colston's town/school historian, keeper of the library archive and trophy case.
- **What she knows:** the raw factual record — old yearbooks, clippings, the composite photos with three retired #4 jerseys spaced a generation apart. She has idly cross-referenced dates before and found it "a funny coincidence," but has never been asked the direct question that would make her say so out loud.
- **What she doesn't know / must not say:** anything about the entity. She is a records-keeper, not someone with hidden knowledge of the mechanism.
- **Wants:** to be helpful and to talk about local history to anyone who'll listen.
- **Fears:** nothing in particular regarding this plot; ordinary pride in her archive.
- **Relationship to the PCs:** friendly, a little starstruck to have "old team boys and girls" back asking about the trophy case.
- **Voice:** chatty, digressive, genuinely delighted by her own archive.
- **Key lines:** "Funny, we don't have a big write-up on Wesley Corbin past that one clipping. Some things just fall out of the paper." / (only if asked directly whether anything like Jesse's case happened before) "Well — now that you mention it..."
- **Stats:** not a combat NPC. No stat block.
- **GM Note:** see `secrets.md`, Secret 3, and `plot.md` Story Point 3. She is a full, fair vector to the historical pattern — do not gate the connection behind an unreasonably hard roll.
