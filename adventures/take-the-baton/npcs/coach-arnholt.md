# Coach Denise Arnholt

Adventure-local. Preparation only until played.

- **Role:** Colston's current head track coach. Named Nora anchor days before the state final.
- **What she knows:** ordinary coaching knowledge and town pride. She has no idea her pep talk echoes Hal Prewitt's 2001 speech almost word for word — she genuinely believes she came up with it herself.
- **What she doesn't know / must not say:** anything about the entity, Jesse, or the pattern. She is not complicit in any conscious sense.
- **Wants:** a clean, honoring win for the town and for Nora specifically; she has staked real professional and personal pride on tonight.
- **Fears:** letting the town down; a scandal or disruption at "her" state final.
- **Relationship to the PCs:** was a year behind them in school; remembers the 2001 team's win as the reason she got into coaching at all.
- **Voice:** warm, confident, a little too pleased with her own pep talk. Turns hard and protective fast if anyone threatens to disrupt the ceremony or pull Nora from the race.
- **Key lines:** "Twenty-five years, and it's like Colston remembered how to want something again." / (at the climax) "Are you out of your mind? She is not stepping off this track."
- **Stats:** see `encounters.md`, "The Anchor Exchange" — Extra, non-lethal social/physical opposition only.
- **GM Note:** she is not a villain and should never be played as one. If shown undeniable proof after the fact, she could become horrified and remorseful — but not in time to help during the climax itself.
