# Diane Vance

Adventure-local. Preparation only until played.

- **Role:** Colston booster club president; runs the pep rally, the retired-jersey ceremony, and most of the anniversary logistics.
- **What she knows:** every detail of "how we've always done it" — the jersey drape, the chant, the bunting at the fence line by Miller's Hole — without ever once asking why.
- **What she doesn't know / must not say:** anything about the entity or the pattern. Her devotion to tradition is sincere and unknowing.
- **Wants:** tonight to go perfectly, exactly the way it "always" does.
- **Fears:** looking unprepared or letting the town's big night fall apart in front of visiting alumni.
- **Relationship to the PCs:** organized their own 25th-anniversary honoring; overly warm and familiar with all of them, especially Nora.
- **Voice:** relentlessly upbeat, a little steamrolling, treats "tradition" as self-evidently good.
- **Key lines:** "Every great one wears it once before the big race. Just for a minute." / (at the climax, physically blocking) "She waited her whole life for this. You don't get to take it from her."
- **Stats:** see `encounters.md`, "The Anchor Exchange" — use the generic Booster Extra profile.
- **GM Note:** she is the clearest voice of the town's unknowing complicity. Play her as certain, not sinister.
