# Nora Sharpe

Adventure-local. Preparation only until played.

- **Role:** Toby Sharpe's daughter, 16, Colston's current 4x400 anchor — just named to the position days before tonight's meet.
- **What she knows:** she's a talented, excited teenager who has no idea anything is happening to her. She feels calmer and more "ready" than she can explain and reads that feeling as confidence, not warning.
- **What she doesn't know / must not say:** everything about the entity, the pattern, and Jesse. She has never heard of Wesley Corbin or Denise Kowalski and has only ever heard "Jesse Calloway" as a name on a retired jersey.
- **Wants:** to run the best race of her life and make her dad proud.
- **Fears:** normal pre-meet nerves, layered under a growing, unnatural calm she doesn't recognize as wrong.
- **Relationship to the PCs:** Toby's daughter; polite, a little starstruck by "Dad's old team" being back in town; doesn't know any of them well personally.
- **Voice:** an ordinary, likable 16-year-old — until a ritual beat lands, when her responses go briefly flat, calm, and slightly too composed, then snap back to normal a moment later.
- **Key lines:** "I feel really good. Really ready." / "I feel so calm. Isn't that good?" / (ordinary teenager, otherwise) "Dad, you're being weird. I've got this."
- **Stats:** not a combat NPC. No stat block; she is never an opponent.
- **GM Note:** her erosion should be played as small and creeping — a beat too calm, a phrase repeated in someone else's cadence — never as an obvious transformation. See `plot.md` Story Points 1, 2, and 6 for the specific escalating beats.
