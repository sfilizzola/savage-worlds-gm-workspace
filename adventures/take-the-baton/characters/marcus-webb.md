## Background (player)

Marcus left Colston for paramedic training right after 2001 and never really came back except for reunions. He's steady, competent, and used to being the person who arrives when something has already gone wrong — which is exactly why it eats at him that he couldn't help Jesse in time to matter.

**Hook:** Jesse pulled him out of a wrecked car the spring before states. Marcus has never repaid it and doesn't know how.

**Stake:** if he can't save Nora, it's the same failure twice.

**The others:** trusts Toby's read of the town without question; doesn't fully know about Renee and Jesse; gets ribbed by Dee for "still being the responsible one."
