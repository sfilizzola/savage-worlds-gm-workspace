## Background (player)

Dee never fully let Colston go, even after moving away — she's spent years on her podcast circling towns with unexplained disappearances without ever quite admitting she was circling her own hometown. Jesse told her things he didn't tell the others. She's always half-known something was wrong here and always talked herself out of saying so.

**Hook:** Jesse's closest confidant — the one person he actually told things to, including about Renee.

**Personal stake:** half-vindication, half-guilt that she never pushed hard enough while it might have mattered.

**The others:** knows things about Renee and Jesse the others don't; the group's natural investigator.
