## Background (player)

Elle kept stats and ran the water table for the 2001 team and never left Colston either — she became the school nurse instead, which puts her at the field most nights the current team practices or competes. She's the natural person to hand a removed player: already on-site, already plausible, already carrying a first-aid kit.

**Hook:** kept the 2001 team's numbers and habits better than anyone; would notice if today's team looked "off" the same way.

**Personal stake:** the same unprocessed history as the other four, seen from just outside the four relay legs.

**The others:** friendly with all four; closer to Toby, since they both stayed.
