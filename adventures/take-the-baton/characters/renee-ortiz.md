## Background (player)

Renee moved out of state right after college and built a careful, orderly life as a teacher. She hasn't been back to Colston in a decade and has never told a living soul that she and Jesse were together — quietly, seriously — for most of that last season. She grieved alone because no one knew there was anything to grieve.

**Hook:** Jesse's secret boyfriend. She's never told anyone, including the other three.

**Personal stake:** a private, unresolved love the town's public memorial can't hold. Seeing him again reopens something she buried on purpose.

**The others:** closest, without saying why, to Dee, the one person Jesse actually confided in; has drifted from Toby and Marcus more than she'd like.
