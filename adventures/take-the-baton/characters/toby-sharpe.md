## Background (player)

Toby never left Colston. He runs the hardware store his dad ran before him and assistant-coaches the current team — partly for Nora, partly because he's never been able to stay away from that field. He's quietly sensed something wrong about Colston for years and told himself it was grief. Tonight, his own daughter is the one wearing the target.

**Hook:** practically brothers with Jesse — roomed together on every away trip.

**Personal stake:** the highest in the room. His daughter Nora is tonight's anchor.

**The others:** closest standing relationship with Hal Prewitt; the one the others lean on for town access and history.
