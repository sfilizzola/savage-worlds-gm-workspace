# Characters

The adventure configuration determines which sections are active. `pc_mode: pregenerated`.

## Party assumptions

- Rank: Novice.
- Player count: 4, with one reserve pregen.
- Allowed character sources: SWADE Fifth Printing (2023) core only. No setting module is active.
- Required competencies without requiring one specific skill/Edge: someone who can physically intervene under pressure (Marcus's medical training and composure), someone with standing in the town who can open doors and read the room (Toby), someone who can dig up buried facts (Dee's research instincts), and someone with outsider distance and Jesse's own private history (Renee's old letters/texts and her clean remove from Colston's social pressure). No single PC is the only route to any essential fact or the only way to attempt the climax.
- Shared reason to engage: all four were on Colston's 2001 4x400 relay squad with Jesse Calloway, back for the 25th-anniversary honoring of that team. All four loved Jesse, in different ways, and none of them have ever really dealt with losing him.

### Marcus Webb

- Concept: paramedic, the one who left and made something steady out of the wreckage.
- Hook: Jesse pulled him out of a wrecked car the spring before states; Marcus has never repaid it and doesn't know how.
- Personal stake: guilt and unfinished debt — if he can't save Nora, it's the same failure twice.
- Party connections: trusts Toby's read of the town; doesn't fully know about Renee and Jesse; needled affectionately by Dee for "still being the responsible one."
- Statistics: `characters/marcus-webb.md`.
- Source verification: SWADE core only; see sheet's build audit.
- Table notes: physical intervention and first aid; not the only PC who can act at the climax.

### Renee Ortiz

- Concept: high school teacher who moved out of state and hasn't been back in a decade.
- Hook: Jesse's secret boyfriend for most of senior year — nobody on the team knew, and she's never told anyone or been able to grieve openly.
- Personal stake: a private, unresolved love the town's public memorial can't hold; seeing Jesse again reopens something she buried on purpose.
- Party connections: closest, without saying why, to Dee, who was the one person Jesse actually told; has drifted from Toby and Marcus more than she'd like.
- Statistics: `characters/renee-ortiz.md`.
- Source verification: SWADE core only; see sheet's build audit.
- Table notes: outsider distance and old texts/letters from Jesse are an independent vector to his state of mind before he vanished; not the only vector.

### Toby Sharpe

- Concept: never left Colston; runs the hardware store and assistant-coaches the current team.
- Hook: practically brothers with Jesse — roomed together on every away trip. His own daughter, Nora, is tonight's anchor.
- Personal stake: the highest in the room. He has quietly sensed something wrong about the town for years and told himself it was grief.
- Party connections: closest standing relationship with Hal Prewitt; the one the others lean on for town access and history.
- Statistics: `characters/toby-sharpe.md`.
- Source verification: SWADE core only; see sheet's build audit.
- Table notes: insider access to Hal Prewitt, Coach Arnholt, and Nora herself; his family relationship should not be the only vector to any essential fact.

### Dee Whitfield

- Concept: true-crime podcaster who has quietly suspected something about Colston for years without proof.
- Hook: Jesse's closest confidant — the one person he actually told things to, including about Renee.
- Personal stake: half-vindication, half-guilt that she never pushed hard enough while it might have mattered.
- Party connections: knows things about Renee and Jesse the others don't; the group's natural investigator.
- Statistics: `characters/dee-whitfield.md`.
- Source verification: SWADE core only; see sheet's build audit.
- Table notes: research and pattern-finding; her prior amateur work can hand the table a free fact in Story Point 3 without gating anything behind her alone.

## Early removal mitigation

- Opening risks that could hard-remove a PC: none by design — Story Points 1–5 are investigative/social, not combat. The climax (Story Point 6) is a social-and-footrace scene, not lethal combat.
- Telegraphing or recovery built into those risks: Coach Arnholt and the boosters are written as obstructive, not violent; escalation to real harm should come only from a PC's own reckless choice (climbing the fence mid-race, provoking a fall), and even then should cost position and time before it costs a PC outright.
- Reserve pregen or allied character: Elle Kim, the 2001 team's manager/stats-keeper, now Colston High's school nurse — present at the field by default.
- Fictional entry point for replacement: Elle is already on-site treating a minor injury or handling first-aid supplies; a removed player's new character can step in within a scene.
- Procedure for capture/separation without sidelining the player: not expected in this adventure's climax design; if the table's choices create it anyway, hand the removed player Elle Kim rather than an NPC extra.

## Later lethality

This adventure is written to stay non-lethal through Story Points 1–5. Real danger is introduced only at the climax (Story Point 6), and only through the PCs' own choices — a fall, a reckless confrontation, or provoking a genuinely dangerous reaction from Jesse or the crowd. Communicate the shift in tone when the state final begins: this is the one place tonight where a bad decision can actually hurt someone. This is risk communication, not immunity.
