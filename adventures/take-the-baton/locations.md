# Locations and Situations

Locations provide actionable state, not predetermined solutions.

## Colston High School Gym

- **Role in adventure:** table start; delivers Story Point 1, the reunion assembly.
- **First impression:** Folding chairs on a scuffed hardwood floor, a hand-painted banner reading "25 YEARS OF COLSTON PRIDE" sagging slightly at one corner, the smell of coffee urns and cut flowers. Late-afternoon light through high windows. The room is loud with reunion chatter, then quiets fast when the principal taps the mic.
- **Current situation:** the joint assembly is underway — the 2001 team honored, this year's team introduced, Nora Sharpe named anchor to a standing cheer.
- **Environment:** packed, warm, exits at both ends and behind the folded-up bleachers; no real cover or privacy for a private conversation without stepping into the hallway.
- **People/forces present:** Principal Auerbach (MC), Coach Denise Arnholt, Hal Prewitt, Jesse's parents (Ruth and Owen Calloway), Diane Vance and other boosters, the current team including Nora, and the whole reunited 2001 squad.
- **What can be learned:** Nora is newly named anchor; the town's collective attention is already, uncomfortably, on both the PCs and Nora at once; something is visibly wrong if a PC looks hard enough.
- **Spoken lines:** see Story Point 1, `plot.md`.
- **Discoverable rolls:** see Story Point 1, `plot.md`.
- **GM-only truth:** Jesse is present at the far end of the gym floor, near the trophy alcove, visible to anyone who looks long enough — almost no one else in the room registers him at all.
- **What changes over time:** the assembly moves to the field within the hour for the pep rally; anything unresolved here can be picked up there.
- **If violence begins:** wildly inappropriate for this scene; if a PC forces a confrontation, treat it as a social rupture (embarrassment, being pulled aside by the principal) rather than combat.
- **Relevant mechanics:** none beyond `Notice` and social Trait tests; no combat expected here.
- **Links:** Story Point 1; leads directly into Colston Field.

## Colston Field and Track

- **Role in adventure:** delivers Story Point 2 (pep rally, jersey ceremony) in the afternoon and Story Point 6 (the state final, climax) that evening. Same physical space, two very different moods.
- **First impression (afternoon, pep rally):** Sun still high over the track's red rubber surface, the stands filling in lawn chairs and bleachers, a PA system crackling with pop music between announcements. Popcorn and cut grass in the air. Someone's already chanting Nora's name before she's even out of the locker room.
- **First impression (evening, state final):** Floodlights turn the infield stark white against a dark sky; the stands are full and loud, a low continuous roar rather than individual voices. The air smells like distant cook-fires from tailgates and the wet-metal smell that comes before a summer storm that never quite arrives. Every eye in the place is on the exchange zone.
- **Current situation (afternoon):** boosters drape Jesse's retired #4 jersey over Nora's shoulders for photos; Coach Arnholt gives a pep talk echoing 2001 almost verbatim.
- **Current situation (evening):** the 4x400 final is running; Nora waits in the exchange zone for the baton on the anchor leg.
- **Environment:** open bleachers, a chain-link perimeter fence, the infield accessible from several gates; crowd noise makes normal conversation hard during the chant and during the race itself.
- **People/forces present:** Coach Arnholt, Diane Vance and boosters, Hal Prewitt (if not yet reached elsewhere), Ruth and Owen Calloway, the current team, and — at the edge of visibility, past the fence line — Jesse.
- **What can be learned:** Nora's stillness during the chant (Story Point 2); at the climax, that Coach Arnholt and the boosters will physically and socially resist any attempt to pull Nora from the race.
- **Spoken lines:** see Story Points 2 and 6, `plot.md`.
- **Discoverable rolls:** see Story Points 2 and 6, `plot.md`.
- **GM-only truth:** the anchor exchange is the exact moment, every generation, that the mark seals. The crowd's chant is not decoration — it is the mechanism.
- **What changes over time:** afternoon becomes evening; whatever groundwork the PCs laid earlier (evidence, allies, a plan) is what they bring into the climax's short clock.
- **If violence begins:** treat as social/physical obstruction from well-meaning people, not lethal combat — a shove, a grabbed arm, a scuffle with a booster, security called. Real danger comes from crowd chaos, a PC doing something reckless (climbing the fence mid-race, provoking a fall), or the entity's environmental presence (flickering lights, wrongness in the chant) rather than an armed opponent.
- **Relevant mechanics:** run the climax intervention as a Dramatic Task or contested Test of Wills per `plot.md` Story Point 6 — use the mechanic the fiction needs, not a scripted fight.
- **Links:** Story Points 2, 6; Miller's Hole (visible past the far fence line on a clear night).

## Colston High School Library and Trophy Case

- **Role in adventure:** delivers Story Point 3, one independent vector to the historical pattern.
- **First impression:** Fluorescent-lit, smelling of old paper and floor wax, a glass trophy case in the front hall crowded with composite team photos going back decades. Three jerseys, numbered 4, hang behind glass at different heights, spaced a generation apart.
- **Current situation:** open to alumni all day for the anniversary; town historian Arlene Combs is on hand, delighted to pull old yearbooks and clippings for "old team boys and girls."
- **Environment:** quiet, private enough for real conversation; microfiche/clipping binders kept in a back room Arlene is happy to unlock.
- **People/forces present:** Arlene Combs (talkative, well-meaning, genuinely doesn't see the pattern she's sitting on).
- **What can be learned:** see Story Point 3, `plot.md` — the 1966 and 1983 disappearances, both anchor runners, both near Miller's Hole, weeks after their wins; the single, buried "winner's curse" reference.
- **Spoken lines:** see Story Point 3, `plot.md`.
- **Discoverable rolls:** see Story Point 3, `plot.md`.
- **GM-only truth:** Arlene has cross-referenced dates before, idly, and found it "a funny coincidence" — she will say this out loud only if asked directly whether anything like this has happened before.
- **What changes over time:** none; available all day.
- **If violence begins:** not applicable.
- **Relevant mechanics:** `Research` or `Notice` per Story Point 3.
- **Links:** Story Point 3; corroborates or is corroborated by Hal Prewitt (Story Point 4) and Jesse (Story Point 5).

## Hal Prewitt's House

- **Role in adventure:** delivers Story Point 4, a human vector to the same truth and a possible ally.
- **First impression:** A small frame house at the edge of town, lawn a little overgrown, blinds mostly drawn even in daylight. A single lamp burns in a front room stacked with newspapers he tells people he means to recycle.
- **Current situation:** Hal isn't at the assembly or the field; he stopped going years ago. He's home, and answers the door only because it's Toby, or because whoever's knocking clearly isn't leaving.
- **Environment:** cramped, private, easy to have an unhurried conversation; a locked desk drawer holds his private clippings.
- **People/forces present:** Hal Prewitt alone.
- **What can be learned:** see Story Point 4, `plot.md` — confirmation Jesse still lingers, and the detail that sightings cluster at the old shaft fence, not the water.
- **Spoken lines:** see Story Point 4, `plot.md`.
- **Discoverable rolls:** see Story Point 4, `plot.md`.
- **GM-only truth:** Hal has never told anyone, including his late wife, what he's seen. Shame, not indifference, has kept him silent.
- **What changes over time:** if pushed too hard early, he shuts the door for the day but can be reached again that evening, more shaken, if the PCs have gathered proof elsewhere first.
- **If violence begins:** not applicable; this is a conversation scene.
- **Relevant mechanics:** `Persuasion` (Spirit) per Story Point 4.
- **Links:** Story Point 4; corroborates Story Points 3 and 5.

## Miller's Hole and the Shaft Fence

- **Role in adventure:** delivers Story Point 5, the most direct (optional) encounter with Jesse and a possible lead for breaking the climax.
- **First impression:** Flat, black water in an old quarry pit, ringed by scrub pine and a rusted chain-link fence sagging at one corner where the ground gave way over the old No. 4 shaft. It's quieter here than it has any right to be a half-mile from a packed stadium — no insects, no wind moving the trees the way it moves everywhere else in town.
- **Current situation:** empty except for whoever comes looking. Jesse can be found here more reliably than anywhere else.
- **Environment:** uneven ground near the fence, deep water close to the bank, poor footing in the dark; no artificial light.
- **People/forces present:** Jesse, present but only intermittently reachable.
- **What can be learned:** see Story Point 5, `plot.md` — that the shaft fence, not just the water, is tied to the ritual, and that part of it is deliberately reopened every anniversary as pep-rally decoration.
- **Spoken lines:** see Story Point 5, `plot.md`. Jesse speaks only in fragments; never write him a monologue.
- **Discoverable rolls:** see Story Point 5, `plot.md`.
- **GM-only truth:** the reopened section of fence is where the booster committee strings the rally's bunting every year, without any of them knowing or asking why that section always seems to need re-fixing.
- **What changes over time:** Jesse is easier to reach here in daylight or early evening than during the climax itself, when his attention (what's left of it) is fixed entirely on Nora.
- **If violence begins:** not applicable; the danger here is drowning risk or a fall near the fence line if a PC is careless, not an attack.
- **Relevant mechanics:** `Spirit` (unskilled) or `Persuasion` per Story Point 5; treat a careless approach to the water's edge in the dark as a plain `Agility` footing check if it comes up, no more.
- **Links:** Story Point 5; visible from Colston Field on a clear night; corroborates Hal Prewitt's account.
