# Encounters and Challenges

Use an encounter only when it makes sense in the fiction. This adventure has no monster to fight. Opposition throughout is Colston's own well-meaning people, acting from love and belief, not malice. Do not reskin any of this as combat.

## The Anchor Exchange (climax)

- **Trigger:** the PCs try to pull Nora from the race, disrupt the crowd's chant, or otherwise interfere once the state final's 4x400 relay has begun (Story Point 6, `plot.md`).
- **Purpose and stakes:** the exact moment the mark seals, every generation. This is the adventure's one required climax.
- **Opposition goal:** Coach Arnholt and the boosters want a clean, perfect night for Nora and for Colston. They will physically and verbally block anything that looks like ruining the ceremony. None of them know what's actually at stake.
- **PC objective:** get Nora out of the exchange zone before or during the anchor leg, or otherwise break the ritual's hold before it seals (see Independent vectors, Story Point 6).
- **Environment and tactical facts:** packed stands, floodlit infield, a short physical clock — the race itself runs under two minutes once the gun fires, and the PCs' real window opens when the baton nears the anchor exchange. The old shaft fence is visible past the far perimeter, a short run from the stands.
- **Scaling:** three or four boosters (Extras) plus Coach Arnholt (Extra) are the acting opposition; do not add more bodies for a larger table — add more insistence and noise instead.
- **Early-removal risk:** mitigated. This is a social-and-footrace scene, not lethal combat; the sharpest physical risk is a fall or a scuffle, not a weapon.
- **Failure consequence:** if the PCs cannot get through, the mark seals. Nothing overtly bad happens at the table — see `plot.md` "Possible end states" for what changes afterward.
- **Exit conditions:** Nora is safely pulled from the moment; the crowd's attention is broken; the shaft fence is disturbed at the right moment; or the exchange completes and the mark seals.
- **Spoken lines:** Coach Arnholt: "Are you out of your mind? She is not stepping off this track." Diane Vance, physically in the way: "She waited her whole life for this. You don't get to take it from her."

### Runtime statistics

**Coach Denise Arnholt** — Extra. Protective, proud, utterly sincere.

- Extra. Pace 6; Parry 5; Toughness 5
- Attributes: Agility d6, Smarts d6, Spirit d8, Strength d6, Vigor d6
- Skills: Athletics d6, Fighting d4, Notice d6, Persuasion d8, Taunt d4
- Edges / Hindrances: none that matter at the table
- Gear: none relevant

**Booster (Diane Vance and others)** — Extra. Use one profile for all boosters.

- Extra. Pace 6; Parry 4; Toughness 5
- Attributes: Agility d6, Smarts d6, Spirit d6, Strength d6, Vigor d6
- Skills: Athletics d4, Fighting d4, Notice d6, Persuasion d6
- Edges / Hindrances: none
- Gear: none relevant

**Mechanic:** treat getting past Coach Arnholt or a booster as a **Test** (SWADE p.107) — an opposed roll using whatever skill fits the PC's approach (`Persuasion` or `Taunt` vs. Smarts or Spirit as appropriate, or a straightforward `Athletics`/`Strength` contest to physically get by), resisted by the NPC's linked attribute. A win lets the PC push past or make the NPC Distracted or Vulnerable; a raise adds Shaken. If the table wants a running structure for the whole exchange rather than single opposed rolls, run it as a **Dramatic Task** (SWADE p.122): three rounds, each PC contributes a relevant roll, and the party needs a set number of successes before the anchor exchange completes (recommend 4–5 tokens across 3 rounds for a table of four).

### Rules verification

- SWADE authority: p.107 (Test); p.122 (Dramatic Tasks); p.34 (Taunt, Persuasion linked Traits); pp.87–88, 95 (Extras)
- Active setting authority: none (`setting_modules: []`)
- House rule: none
- Adaptation notes: no invented subsystem. Use whichever of Test or Dramatic Task better fits how the table is playing the scene; do not run both for the same beat.

### Difficulty review

- Expected pressure: social, not violent. The clock (the race itself) is the real pressure, not the NPCs' combat capability.
- Volatility or swing risk: low — none of the opposition is meant to seriously hurt a PC. If a player escalates to real violence against Coach Arnholt or a booster, let it land as a serious social and legal consequence (assault at a state championship, on camera), not as a fight worth stat-blocking further.
- Resource drain: Bennies for the Test/Dramatic Task rolls; no ammo, no Wounds expected unless a PC does something reckless (climbing the fence, shoving through a packed crowd).
- What changes at minimum and maximum player count: at three PCs, drop the token requirement for the Dramatic Task to 3–4; at five, raise it to 5–6. Do not add more named opposition either way — add more crowd noise and time pressure instead.

## The Crowd's Chant (pressure, not opposition)

- **Trigger:** Story Point 2 (pep rally) and Story Point 6 (climax), whenever the crowd's collective chant peaks.
- **Purpose and stakes:** this is the ritual's actual mechanism, made audible. It is atmosphere and information, not a monster to fight.
- **Opposition goal:** n/a — no one is attacking.
- **PC objective:** notice and interpret what's happening to Nora; this is an observation beat, not a combat beat.
- **Environment and tactical facts:** a packed crowd, floodlights, sound that seems to layer and thicken for a few seconds at each peak.
- **Scaling:** n/a.
- **Early-removal risk:** none.
- **Failure consequence:** none story-stopping; see Story Points 2 and 6, `plot.md`, for the relevant Discoverable rolls.
- **Exit conditions:** n/a — this is a recurring beat, not a resolved encounter.
- **Spoken lines:** none inherent; whatever NPCs are present in that story point may speak per `plot.md`.

**Optional Fear check:** if a PC witnesses Jesse do something more overt than standing and watching (for instance, a sudden, wrong stillness breaking into a single desperate motion toward Nora), that is grotesque/uncanny enough to warrant a **Fear check** (SWADE pp.123–124) — a free Spirit roll. Use sparingly, no more than once per session, and only for the single most uncanny beat the table actually reaches. This is dread, not a monster attack; do not stack multiple Fear checks across the night.

### Rules verification

- SWADE authority: pp.123–124 (Fear Checks, Fear Table)
- Active setting authority: none
- House rule: none
- Adaptation notes: use at most once; this is not a creature with a stated Fear rating, so treat any Fear check here as a GM's call for the single most appropriate beat, not a mechanical trigger tied to a stat block.
