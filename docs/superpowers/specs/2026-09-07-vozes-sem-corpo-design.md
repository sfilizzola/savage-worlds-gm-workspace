# Vozes sem Corpo — Adventure Design

Approved in conversation on 2026-09-07. Repository and table material are in English; the Portuguese title remains the campaign's established title.

## Contract

The adventure begins the morning after *Neblina sobre o Lago*. Chief Whitley shelved Frank Loman's file only hours ago, and Abigail Carr's call ended the previous session. Frank's body is still hidden near the tower because the two-man crew has not had time to complete its cleanup.

**Premise:** The morning after Whitley shelves Frank Loman, Lilly goes to 102.3 FM about Abigail's recording and finds a purported engineer already there for the reel. She must learn what happened to Frank and preserve enough independent evidence to prevent the file from being honestly dismissed as a drowning before the crew moves his body and erases the original records.

**Player-facing mission:** Hear the tape, find Frank, and build a file that cannot honestly be dismissed as a drowning.

**Resolution boundary:** Lilly can prove that Frank interrupted a local signal capture/replay job, was killed, and was staged as a walk into Lake Barrow. Independent records share one precise missing interval. Muddy local paperwork does not establish ClearWave, a national consortium, or the Correction.

## Predetermined truths

- There is no supernatural event. Lilly's name on the radio was captured local police traffic replayed through the crew's equipment.
- Frank interrupted a two-man capture/replay job near the lakeside tower.
- Cal Briggs killed Frank during the confrontation. Ray Holtz helped stage the lake scene and works the signal equipment.
- Frank's body is in an inland utility outbuilding at the tower compound, not in the lake.
- The barefoot tracks and mud cassette are staging leftovers. The cassette is work media from the capture job.
- Abigail's reel contains ordinary programming, a precise missing interval, then bleed from local police traffic.
- The station reel and dispatch recorder share the same missing interval. A maintenance timing log and the mud cassette provide additional independent vectors.
- Local access paperwork points through a Water Authority work order, a continuity-maintenance description, an Eugene callback, and a PO box that does not resolve cleanly. ClearWave is not named.
- Ashgrove is tiny. Two unfamiliar men and an out-of-county truck draw notice. The crew splits because appearing together again would become a town story.

## People

- **Abigail Carr:** practical, rattled radio professional who wants the recording heard and her job protected. She knows only what the station captured.
- **Ray Holtz:** capture operator. At the opening he uses a maintenance cover to obtain the original reel. He fears the tape and body becoming one murder file.
- **Cal Briggs:** driver and muscle. He killed Frank, staged the prints, and waits at the compound to move the body and wipe the equipment.
- **Helen Loman:** Frank's sister. She can establish his habits and reject the idea that he deliberately walked into the lake; she knows nothing about the tower job.
- **Sgt. Doyle:** heard the lake sound and supports Lilly when her facts match his experience.
- **Chief Whitley:** wants the new detective, the station, and the missing fisherman to stop becoming a public spectacle.
- **Nancy and ordinary townspeople:** provide a human social map. They notice outsiders but do not know the conspiracy.
- **Water Authority clerk:** can distinguish real local procedure from the vague work order but cannot explain the capture equipment.

## Shape and mix

Target a 3–5 hour solo one-shot, with approximately 70% investigation/puzzles and 30% action.

1. **Tape in motion:** Lilly reaches 102.3 FM while Ray is already trying to take the reel. The first decision concerns custody, copying, or following him.
2. **The missing hour:** station reel, dispatch audio, maintenance timing, and the mud cassette can be compared without making one roll or one object mandatory.
3. **Two outsiders:** Ashgrove's social network identifies two unfamiliar men, their truck, and the fact they separated this morning.
4. **Paper that does not land:** local paperwork proves access but leads to a dead callback and muddy ownership.
5. **One reversal:** once alerted, Cal begins wiping the compound and preparing to move Frank.
6. **Tower compound:** one climactic action situation. Opposition, wet metal, live equipment, darkness, and a cleanup clock combine with a secondary objective: preserve Frank's body or an original record.
7. **Closure:** Lilly can answer Frank's case and identify the repeated missing interval without discovering the campaign's hidden organization.

Story points are reachable situations, not a mandatory order. The player may follow Ray, work records first, use town witnesses, or go directly toward the tower.

## Failure and endings

- Losing the original station reel leaves copies, dispatch audio, the mud cassette, witnesses, and timing records.
- Missing Cal's cleanup can cost the body, equipment, or an original log, but not every route to the truth.
- Full success preserves Frank, an original signal record, and the local access trail.
- Costly success proves murder but loses the clean synchronization comparison or lets one crewman escape.
- Partial success establishes the staged lake and repeated gap while the official disposition remains contested.
- Failure lets the crew move Frank and strengthens Whitley's drowning narrative, but Lilly retains whatever independent evidence she protected.

Night 3 remains an unrelated action case. Preparation does not become campaign history until play establishes it.
