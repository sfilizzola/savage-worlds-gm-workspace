# House Rules

No house rules are active by default.

Every house rule must be explicitly approved by the GM and assigned a stable ID. Adventures activate house rules by ID; writing `none` is required when none apply.

## Template

```md
### HR-001 - Short name

- Status: proposed | active | retired
- Approved by:
- Approved on:
- Scope:
- Overrides: source title, page/section
- Rule:
- Reason:
- Known consequences:
- Adventures using it:
```

Proposals are not active rules. Retired rules remain in the record with their retirement date.

### HR-NFA-001 - Fifteen skill points (Lilly / No Further Action)

- Status: active
- Approved by: GM (campaign design 2026-09-07)
- Approved on: 2026-09-07
- Scope: Player character Lilly Dawson in `campaigns/no-further-action/` at creation and when rebuilding era sheets, unless a later ruling says otherwise.
- Overrides: SWADE Fifth Printing skill-point budget at creation (core rules character creation).
- Rule: Lilly is built with **15 skill points** after core skills at d4, not the standard 12. Attribute and Hindrance/Edge budgets stay SWADE-normal (Human Edge; two Major Hindrances funding two additional Edges).
- Reason: Player-supplied 1986 sheet already uses this budget.
- Known consequences: She is slightly more competent at Novice than a 12-point detective. Do not silently give other PCs the same budget if a second character appears.
- Adventures using it: `campaigns/no-further-action/` (all children unless excepted).

### HR-NFA-002 - Noir investigation procedures (SWADE names)

- Status: active
- Approved by: GM (campaign design 2026-09-07)
- Approved on: 2026-09-07
- Scope: `campaigns/no-further-action/` only. Detective procedure at the table. Not a setting module.
- Overrides: none of SWADE’s core resolution. Adds optional procedures when the fiction is long research, a held interrogation, talking a room down, or an unnoticed tail.
- Rule: Use *Deadlands Noir* (PEG, 2012) **Detective Work** tools only, converted to SWADE Traits: **Hitting the Books** uses **Research** (printed p. 29); **Interrogations** are a three-round Social Conflict using Intimidation, Persuasion, or Taunt (printed p. 31); **Patter** for talking people aside (printed p. 32); **Tailing** Stealth/Driving vs Notice (printed pp. 32–33). Failures cost time, money, heat, or position — they do not brick the campaign. Cite the book at the table; do not paste long rules text into adventures. **Do not use:** Magic, Harrowed, Fear Levels, Noir Poverty, Blood & Guts, Gritty Damage, Knock-Out Blows, Second Wind, or anything from *Deadlands Noir Companion* as setting. Old-edition skills (Investigation, Streetwise, Lockpicking) are not added; use SWADE Research, Persuasion, Thievery, and so on.
- Reason: Solo mysteries need procedures so one failed Notice does not stop the night; the campaign is not Deadlands.
- Known consequences: Interrogation and Patter need someone who cannot (or will not) simply leave. Tailing is not a Chase unless she is burned.
- Adventures using it: `campaigns/no-further-action/` (all children unless excepted).
