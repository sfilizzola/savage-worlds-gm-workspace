### RUL-2026-08-29-01 - Steel helmets (WW2 → SWADE)

- Adventure/session: Operation Hinterland
- Question: Weird War II p.14 lists a steel helmet as Armor +2 *and* a 50% chance to protect against a head shot. SWADE uses Armor and Called Shots, not a percentile save.
- Sources checked: Weird War II p.14; SWADE Fifth Printing (2023) p.65 (Armor hits the torso unless a Called Shot), pp. 98–99 (Called Shot head/vitals −4, +4 damage; face of an open-faced helmet −5 bypasses the helmet)
- Decision: Steel helmets are Armor +2 vs Called Shots to the head only. The 50% save is not used.
- Reason: Convert the printed Armor value into SWADE; drop the Explorer-era extra.
- Scope: this adventure
- Promote to house rule: no
