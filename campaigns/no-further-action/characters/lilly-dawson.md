## Background (player-facing; not the Correction)

New Jersey; parents Medrick and Simon Dawson; younger sister Ashley. Simon is not her biological father; that fact came through a PI she hired as a teenager. The biological father is a convicted violent offender. She became a cop in part for the work and in part for access. Long-term professional goal: FBI. Personal fear: that her father’s violence is in her.

Lilly has never contacted or met her biological father. Everything she believes about his crimes comes from the investigator's findings and official records.

## Promotion and transfer

On **10 January 1986**, Lilly receives what she wanted: promotion to **Detective Third Class**. The order also sends her from New Jersey to the small Ashgrove Police Department in Oregon under a nominal interdepartmental cooperation initiative.

The assignment is informal discipline disguised as opportunity. On an unrelated New Jersey case, Lilly pushed past instructions, violated protocol, and continued investigating after her superiors wanted the matter left alone. They consider her talented and do not want a formal punishment on her record; they want distance and obedience.

Captain **Harold M. Keane**, Investigations Division, sends a private note making the bargain clear: the promotion is real, but Ashgrove is a cooling-off assignment. Do the requested work, stop disturbing hornets' nests, prove she can work inside the chain of command, and the Bureau may still be possible.

Lilly does not want to go. Refusing an official promotion would confirm that she cannot accept orders and could end her FBI ambition. She intends to serve temporarily, prove them wrong, and return east with detective experience.

From Lilly's perspective, Ashgrove is simply the distant small department that accepted the arrangement. She has no reason to suspect a family or investigative connection.
