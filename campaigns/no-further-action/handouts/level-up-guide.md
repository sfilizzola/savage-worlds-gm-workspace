# Level-up guide — Lilly Dawson (for the player)

One-page cheat sheet for spending Lilly's first Advance, at the start of Night 3.
Not table canon. Not a briefing. Not session history — this is a rules explainer only.

---

## What "leveling up" means in Savage Worlds

There's no XP bar. At the GM's call — usually once per session in a short campaign
— your character earns one **Advance**. Advances add up into **Rank**:

| Advances | Rank |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Novice |
| 4–7 | Seasoned |
| 8–11 | Veteran |
| 12–15 | Heroic |
| 16+ | Legendary |

Lilly is starting Night 3 with **her first Advance**. That keeps her solidly
**Novice** (1 of the 0–3 band) — no rebuild, no new Rank yet. Higher Rank (Seasoned,
for 1998) only happens when the campaign moves eras.

*Authority: SWADE Fifth Printing (2023), p.54, "Advancement" / "Rank".*

---

## The five things one Advance can buy

Pick **one** of these. A Trait can never go above its ancestral max (d12 for a human).

1. **A new Edge** — any Edge Lilly currently qualifies for (see below).
2. **Raise one skill by one die step** — but only a skill that's *already equal to or
   above* its linked attribute.
3. **Raise two skills by one die step each** — skills that are *below* their linked
   attribute. One of the two can be a brand-new skill, starting at d4.
4. **Raise one attribute by one die step.** You can only do this **once per Rank** —
   so this is a one-time offer while Lilly is Novice.
5. **Soften a Hindrance** — erase a Minor Hindrance, or knock a Major down to Minor.
   (Removing a Major entirely takes *two* saved-up Advances, not one.)

*Authority: SWADE Fifth Printing (2023), p.54, "Advancement".*

## Option 1 — a new Edge, worked examples

Edges she **already qualifies for right now**, no other changes needed:

- **Quick** *(Agility d8+)* — bad Action Cards (5 or less) get discarded and redrawn.
- **Brave** *(Spirit d6+)* — +2 on Fear checks, −2 on the Fear Table result.
- **Calculating** *(Smarts d8+)* — on a low Action Card (5 or less), ignore up to 2 points
  of penalty on one action that turn.
- **Martial Artist** *(Fighting d6+)* — fists/feet count as weapons: +1 to hit, Strength+d4 damage unarmed.
- **Trademark Weapon** *(a weapon skill d8+)* — her service Glock, since Shooting is d8:
  +1 to Shooting with it, +1 Parry when it's readied.
- **Free Runner** *(Agility d8+, Athletics d6+)* — full Pace over difficult ground on
  foot; +2 Athletics when climbing or in a foot chase.
- **Luck** *(no requirement)* — one extra Benny at the start of every session.

Edges she does **not** qualify for yet (need an attribute raised first):
Hard to Kill (Spirit d8+), Nerves of Steel / Iron Jaw (Vigor d8+), Brawler / Sweep
(Strength d8+ **and** Vigor d8+).

*Authority: SWADE Fifth Printing (2023), pp.37–45, Background/Combat Edges.*

## Option 2 — raise a skill at/above its attribute

Qualifying skills right now: **Athletics d8** (Agility d8), **Shooting d8** (Agility d8),
**Research d8** (Smarts d8) — any one of these could go to **d10**.

Example: Shooting d8 → d10. She's simply a better shot, no other change.

## Option 3 — raise two skills below their attribute

Any two skills sitting under their linked attribute go up one step each. Examples:

- **Fighting d6 → d8** and **Stealth d4 → d6** (both under Agility d8).
- **Persuasion d4 → d6** and **Intimidation d4 → d6** (both under Spirit d6).
- Or bring in a brand-new skill at d4 (e.g. Battle, Performance) as one of the two.

## Option 4 — raise an attribute (once-per-Rank offer)

- **Vigor d4 → d6** — tougher, and a step closer to the Vigor d8+ Edges above.
- **Spirit d6 → d8** — a step closer to Hard to Kill, Charismatic, Elan.

Remember: this is the *only* attribute raise she'll be offered until she reaches
Seasoned — the other four options can be taken again on future Advances, this one can't.

## Option 5 — soften a Hindrance

She has two Majors, no Minors, so this Advance would knock one Major down to Minor:

- **Night Terrors** → Minor (the nightmares ease, they don't stop).
- **Curious** → Minor (she still can't leave a thread alone, just less recklessly).

A full removal of either Major would need this Advance *plus* a future one banked together.
