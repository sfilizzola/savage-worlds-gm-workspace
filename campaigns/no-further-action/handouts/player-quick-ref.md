# SAVAGE WORLDS — PLAYER CARD

Keep this beside your sheet. The GM has the book. You do not need it.

**You are a Wild Card.** You roll an extra **Wild Die (d6)** with every Trait. You can take **three Wounds**. You start with **3 Bennies**.

---

## How to roll

Roll the die on your sheet **and** a d6 (Wild Die). Keep the **higher**. Both can **Ace**: max on the die → roll again and add.

- **Success:** 4 or more after modifiers (Target Number 4 unless the GM says otherwise).
- **Raise:** every 4 over the Target Number. One raise is the extra effect (better information, +1d6 damage on an attack, and so on).
- **Opposed:** you still need a 4. Then they must match or beat you. Raises are counted from *their* total.
- **Unskilled:** no skill on the sheet → **d4−2** (and the Wild Die).
- **Critical Failure:** 1 on **both** Trait die and Wild Die. It fails badly. You **cannot** Benny this.

Typical modifiers: easy **+2**, hard **−2**, very hard **−4**. Wounds and Fatigue also subtract.

---

## Bennies (3)

Tokens. Spend them. Leftovers vanish at the end of the night.

- **Reroll a Trait.** Keep the best total. Critical Failure still stands.
- **Un-Shake instantly** (even off-turn).
- **Soak:** after damage, before Wounds land. Vigor; each success and raise soaks **one Wound** from that hit. Soak them all and you are not Shaken either. Once per attack; you may Benny the Vigor roll.
- **New Action Card** (in a fight, after cards are dealt).
- **Reroll damage.**
- **Nudge the story** — only if the GM allows it (a small item, a bit more talk, a missed detail).

A **Joker** on your Action Card: you act whenever you want this round, **+2** to Trait and damage, and **every player** gets a Benny.

---

## Your turn (about 6 seconds)

Cards: Ace down to 2. Ties: Spades, Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs. Joker goes when it wants.

On your card you may **move Pace 6″ (12 yards)** and take **one action**. Movement is not an action. Climb, crawl, swim, or rough ground: each inch costs **2″**.

**Run:** add a d6 to Pace this turn (does not Ace). **−2** to all your actions.

**Multi-action:** up to **three** actions. Two actions = **−2** to both. Three = **−4** to all three. Declare them before you roll.

**Free (no penalty):** a short sentence, drop something, go prone, stand (−2″ Pace), recover from Shaken/Stunned (once, start of turn), ready **two** familiar items.

**Hold:** wait. To interrupt someone, opposed **Athletics**. If you are Shaken or Stunned on Hold, you lose it.

## Hitting and hurting

| You try | Roll | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| Fists, a grab, a blade | **Fighting** | their **Parry** |
| Her service pistol | **Shooting** | **4**, plus range |

**Range** (from the weapon on your sheet): Short —, Medium **−2**, Long **−4**. 1″ = 2 yards.

**Raise on the attack:** +1d6 damage (once, even with extra raises). Damage dice Ace. Melee damage is Strength + weapon — **no Wild Die**.

Then compare damage to **Toughness**:

- Less than Toughness → nothing that counts.
- Equal or higher → **Shaken**.
- Each **raise** on damage → a **Wound** (and Shaken).
- Already Shaken + another Shaken from **physical damage** → a Wound, still Shaken.

**Wild Cards (you):** three Wounds, then Incapacitated. Each Wound: **−1** Pace (min 1″) and **−1** to Trait rolls (max **−3**).

**Extras** (most people she meets): **one Wound** and they are out.

**Shaken:** you may only move and take free actions (you can still Run). Start of your turn: **Spirit** as a free action — success and you act normally. Or spend a Benny anytime.

**Called Shot:** a limb is **−2**; the head or vitals are **−4** and **+4** damage. Rarely worth it unless you need the extra damage.

**Cover:** −2 / −4 / −6 / −8 (quarter / half / three-quarters / barely visible).

## Detective work (house rule HR-NFA-002)

Lilly's cases run on these procedures instead of a single "Investigation" skill. Failure costs time, money, heat, or position — it does not brick the case.

- **Hitting the Books** — long research, records, or background digging: **Research**. Success finds the basics; a raise finds more. A Critical Failure can turn up plausible but wrong information.
- **Interrogation** — a held subject who cannot simply leave: a three-round Social Conflict using **Intimidation**, **Persuasion**, or Taunt-style pressure, opposed by the subject's Spirit (or Smarts, GM's call).
- **Patter** — talking a room down or getting someone to open up outside a formal interrogation: **Persuasion**.
- **Tailing** — following someone without being made: **Stealth** or **Driving** opposed by their **Notice**.

Investigator (Edge, on the sheet) can apply to Notice or Research when the fiction supports it.

## Combat tricks (when it matters)

- **Aim** (whole turn, stay put): next turn's first shot ignores 4 points of range/cover, or +2 to hit.
- **Defend** (whole turn, no extra actions, no Run): +4 Parry until your next turn.
- **Wild Attack** (melee): +2 Fighting and damage; you are Vulnerable until the end of your next turn.
- **The Drop** (target bound or truly unaware): +4 attack and damage for one action.
- **Withdraw** from someone adjacent: they get a free attack against you.
- **Distracted:** −2 to your Trait rolls until the end of your next turn. **Vulnerable:** others add +2 to actions against you until then.
