# Vozes sem Corpo — Investigation Evidence Handouts

English source of truth for the six approved in-play evidence props. These are prepared discoveries, not established history. The table adaptation is [`investigation-evidence.pt.md`](investigation-evidence.pt.md); the cut-apart print packet is [`print/investigation-evidence.pt.html`](print/investigation-evidence.pt.html).

Print the packet single-sided. Separate its pieces before play and hand over only the evidence Lilly actually obtains. A failed roll never makes an essential fact disappear: use the corresponding spoken testimony or slower comparison from `plot.md`.

RUN table IDs: **Handout A** is Lilly's notebook (separate print). This packet is **Handouts B–G**. Reminders live in each story point of `plot.md` and `RUN.md`, plus the Handouts index in `RUN.md`.

## Delivery order

1. **Water Authority work order** (**Handout B**) — visible on Abigail's desk at KCRK. Ray is using it to claim the original reel.
2. **KCRK program log** (**Handout C**) — Abigail supplies it when she cooperates. Counter readings locate the cut; the written clock times measure the interval.
3. **Dispatch recorder log** (**Handout D**) — available through ordinary police access when Lilly compares independent records.
4. **Town Hall key register** (**Handout E**) — supplied by the public-works clerk or found through records work.
5. **West End Fuel carbon slip** (**Handout F**) — supplied by Bud Ellison or recovered from the carbon pad.
6. **TDK cassette label and evidence tag** (**Handout G**) — optional. Use only if Lilly seeks the mud cassette. Final-run default: precinct property area, incomplete seal/date fields; honor a player's explicit memory of different custody.

Do not hand the entire packet to the player at once.

## 1. Water Authority continuity work order

This found document remains English in the pt-BR table packet. Its body is an old purple spirit-duplicator form. `CALLBACK` and `BILL TO` are later black electric-typewriter additions, slightly misaligned. It does not contain a station equipment identifier.

```text
WATER AUTHORITY
CONTINUITY MAINTENANCE — FIELD WORK ORDER

WORK ORDER: 86-011
SITE: T-2
SERVICE: CONTINUITY MAINTENANCE
CREW: 2
VEHICLE: COUNTY UTILITY

ACCESS:
Obtain local service key. Maintain continuity during isolation.
Return key after completion.

AUTHORIZED SIGNATURE: ____________________
DATE: ____________________

CALLBACK: (503) 687-4419
 BILL TO: P.O. BOX 2144
          EUGENE, OR 97401
```

The exact work-order number is administrative texture only and is not an investigative key.
