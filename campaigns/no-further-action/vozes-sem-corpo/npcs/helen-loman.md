# Helen Loman

- **Canon status:** proposed adventure-local person; Frank's sibling relationship becomes established only through play
- **Visibility:** player-facing
- **Role:** Frank Loman's sister and habit witness
- **Current objective:** make police keep looking and stop strangers from turning Frank into a convenient story
- **Fear:** that Frank will be called a drunk, runaway, or drowning because nobody found him
- **Leverage/resources:** knowledge of Frank's routines, boots, fishing habits, drinking, and promises; willingness to identify his effects
- **Secret:** none; she knows nothing about the tower job
- **Relationships:** protective of Frank; distrustful of Whitley's quick closure; judges Lilly by whether she asks specific questions
- **If ignored:** calls the precinct again and tells neighbors the new detective gave up
- **What can change their behavior:** concrete questions, visible evidence handling, honesty about what Lilly does not yet know

## Portrayal

- **First impression:** sleepless, coat buttoned wrong, answers practical questions before emotional ones
- **Voice/manner cue:** corrects verbs and habits; refuses soft euphemisms
- **Spoken lines:**
  - “Frank took his boots off at home. He didn't walk cold mud barefoot for anybody.”
  - “He might miss supper. He wouldn't leave the car open and make me guess.”
  - “Don't tell me the lake took him if you know it didn't.”
- **Avoid:** knowing Ray, Cal, the tower operation, the recording, or Frank's unseen final confrontation
- **Pressure variants:**
  - **Cooperate:** “Ask me one thing at a time.”
  - **Refuse:** “Not if you're writing ‘drowning’ before you find him.”
  - **Threatened:** Stands, opens the door, and says, “We're done.”
  - **Ignored:** Calls again, then tells Nancy and Daisy’s that police stopped looking.
