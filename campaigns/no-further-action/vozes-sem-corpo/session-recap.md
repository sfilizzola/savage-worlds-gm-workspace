# Session 2 - Vozes sem Corpo

- **Date played:** 20–21 September 2026 (table); January 1986, the wet morning after Session 1
- **Adventure:** `campaigns/no-further-action/vozes-sem-corpo/`
- **Players/PCs present:** Lilly Dawson (solo)
- **Starting state:** Whitley had shelved Frank Loman hours earlier. Abigail Carr’s call about the recording was unanswered. Frank still missing.
- **Ending state:** Frank found dead at the inland tower compound. The Loman death file is closed by Lilly and Doyle and signed by Whitley. Ray Holtz dead at the scene. Cal Briggs died of a shoulder wound in the ambulance. What the two men were doing — who sent them, what the equipment was for — is **not** established. Lilly still finds the radio voice odd. A shared hole exists in the registers; the exact duration was never noticed. Nausea at the lake (Session 1) and at the tower remains unexplained in play.

## What actually happened

Lilly went into town first, then to **KCRK**. **Ray Holtz** was already there after the tape. Her presence intimidated him; he left without taking the recordings, complaining that **his boss would be furious**. Lilly and **Abigail Carr** came out of the studio on good terms — a real working rapport, not only a handoff of evidence.

**Daisy’s**, **West End Fuel**, and **Town Hall** (public-works paper / access) were essential. Those routes pointed her to the **inland utility tower** off the lake road — not the station, not a drowning in Lake Barrow.

She arrived at the compound **alone**. Ray and Cal were **moving Frank’s body onto the truck**. **Cal opened fire** as soon as he saw her. No arrest attempt: the standoff went to guns. The two men shot poorly; Cal **missed Lilly several times**. She meant to incapacitate. She shot **Ray** in the standoff and was not precise enough — Ray died there. She hit **Cal** in the **shoulder**; he blacked out. He **bled out in the ambulance** on the way to hospital and died.

After the shooting she called **backup on the police radio**. **Sgt. Doyle** processed the scene with her. They collected evidence and **closed Frank’s death together**. The body was in the act of being loaded. Lilly examined it and **saw the head injury**. In the pickup she found **Cal’s work flashlight** with marks and dents. That is physical suggestion, not a table finding of who swung the blow: both subjects were already dead when the scene was worked.

Doyle, known in Ashgrove, **spoke with and comforted Helen Loman** (Frank’s people / sister). Lilly had reached Doyle by radio at the end of the fight.

They compared **all the registers** they had. Each showed **the same hole**. Nobody marked a precise length (the prepared 1 minute 52 seconds / 9:14–9:15 window was **not** noticed at the table). Lilly **never** connected the odd voice to captured police radio replayed through the tower gear. She still thinks the voice is strange.

Whitley **signed** the close. When Lilly handed the papers he told her: **“Nice work, kid.”** The official story of Frank is a closed death case: not a drowning; he died in connection with two outsiders working around the lake on **fake paperwork**; he was **too curious at the wrong time**. The file does **not** say what the job was or who employed them.

Lilly kept pulling the **nausea** and the interference. She talked to some of **Frank’s friends** who had been **at the lake at the time of the gap** (out looking, tied to the disappearance). They had felt nauseated too. They chose to **blame Daisy’s food**. **Daisy Pell was furious**. Lilly knows that explanation is wrong.

At the compound she **climbed the tower** and felt a little nausea, then — as if someone had **turned something off** once the police situation was obvious — the sickness **stopped**.

## Decisions and consequences

- Player decision: Worked town (Daisy’s, fuel, Town Hall) until the inland tower was findable; went in alone; shot to stop, not to kill, and accepted lethal results; called Doyle after; closed the death file; kept asking about nausea and the voice.
- Immediate consequence: Both crewmen dead; Frank recovered; Whitley will sign a close rather than a drowning shelf; Abigail is an ally at the station; Daisy is angry about the food rumor; Helen was met by Doyle, not left as a rumor.
- Future consequence: Night 3 remains an unrelated job months later. The two outsiders, fake paper, the shared gap, the unsolved voice, and the nausea are live texture — not a named organization. Ray’s “boss” is only something he said.
