# Encounters and Challenges

The investigation uses ordinary Trait tests and optional HR-NFA-002 procedures. The compound uses standard combat only if Lilly's choices produce it. No setting module is active.

## Reel Custody and a Possible Tail

- **Trigger:** Lilly lets Ray leave, with or without the reel, and follows him.
- **Purpose and stakes:** learn where he goes without immediately alerting Cal.
- **Opposition goal:** Ray wants to notice a tail, make a warning call, and leave town.
- **PC objective:** maintain contact or identify his destination/communication.
- **Environment and tactical facts:** wet streets, sparse traffic, a town where an unfamiliar truck and a police car are both conspicuous.
- **Scaling:** one opposed route for one Novice Wild Card.
- **Early-removal risk:** none.
- **Failure consequence:** Ray spots Lilly and warns Cal; Lilly still learns his direction or warning location. The warning triggers active cleanup, but does not itself erase a category before elapsed time or a compound round advances it.
- **Exit conditions:** Ray reaches the compound, makes a warning call, loses Lilly, or is lawfully detained.
- **Spoken lines:** If confronted, Ray says, “You following me or arresting me? Pick one.”

## Tower Compound Preservation

- **Trigger:** Lilly enters, observes, or confronts the compound, either before or after Cal is alerted.
- **Purpose and stakes:** preserve Frank's body and at least one original record before or while two crewmen attempt erasure and escape.
- **Opposition goal:** Cal prepares to move Frank; once alerted, he destroys records and accelerates movement. Ray keeps Lilly away from the rack long enough for the record to be destroyed, then escapes without being attached to the killing.
- **PC objective:** choose what to protect, contain, or pursue. Defeating both men is neither necessary nor sufficient.
- **Environment and tactical facts:** wet concrete/metal, low light, chain-link, truck, outbuilding, breakers, live rack, cold burning barrel/stove, two exits. If alerted, the fire/wipe become active. Visible power isolation can stop a wipe but may destroy a live comparison if done carelessly.
- **Scaling:** Cal and Ray are Extras. Ray may be absent, delayed, detained, or willing to separate from Cal based on earlier choices. For a hurt or resource-depleted Lilly, begin Ray outside the fight or have Doyle arrive after one cleanup step if she requested support. Do not add enemies.
- **Early-removal risk:** mitigated by telegraphed lethal danger, escape-focused opposition, and surrender/retreat exits.
- **Failure consequence:** each missed cleanup step costs one evidence category; already protected evidence remains. If Lilly is incapacitated, Doyle's delayed recovery creates a degraded evidence state rather than erasing all progress.
- **Exit conditions:** body/truck secured; wipe stopped; crew surrender, flee, or are incapacitated; Lilly retreats with evidence; cleanup completes.
- **Spoken lines:** Cal: “Badge stays outside. Turn around.” Ray: “Back away from the rack. You don't know what you're touching.”

### Compound states and cleanup sequence

**Unalerted:** Cal is preparing Frank and the truck for movement. The paper log, active signal head, and body remain intact and unmoved. Give Lilly an observation/intervention beat. A failed approach observation lets Cal detect her after that observation; a success identifies useful features; a raise preserves surprise through one immediate action. No cleanup step resolves until Cal is warned or detects police attention.

**Alerted:** Cal begins active destruction and movement. This transition is the adventure's one major reversal. Ray's warning, town/formal attention reaching the crew, or Cal detecting Lilly can trigger it.

Once alerted, advance one step at the end of each combat round, or after each substantial unopposed delay. The alert itself starts the clock but does not erase a category. Skip a step already prevented.

1. Cal destroys the paper machine log.
2. Ray or the automatic cycle erases the active signal head.
3. Cal starts the truck with Frank loaded or begins loading him if Lilly blocked the truck.
4. Any crewman with an open route escapes.

Lilly can interrupt a step through any fictional action that actually prevents it. Use the relevant core Trait; failure costs position or permits the step, rather than inventing a bespoke subsystem.

### Rules verification

- **SWADE authority:** pp.10–11 derived statistics; pp.29 Athletics; pp.87–88 Wild Cards and Extras; p.95 one Wound incapacitates an Extra; p.102 illumination; p.202 creating NPCs.
- **Active setting authority:** none.

- **Adaptation notes:** both opponents are custom civilian Extras under SWADE NPC guidance. They have no firearms, combat Edges, armor, or invented improvised-weapon values. If a tool becomes a weapon: `RULE UNCLEAR - GM DECISION REQUIRED`.

### At-hand environment

- **Low light:** use the actual illumination category; if the compound is Dark, apply −4 to affected attacks, Notice, and similar tasks and the visibility limit in SWADE p.102. A flashlight or lit work area may make a local zone Dim instead.
- **Wet footing:** call for `Athletics` only when an action meaningfully crosses slick height or moving machinery. Failure leaves Lilly prone or out of position; do not add invented damage.
- **Live equipment:** `Electronics` determines careful preservation. A success secures the head or stops an active wipe; a raise also preserves an independently timed machine state. On failure while alerted, the live head is lost. On failure while unalerted, Cal is alerted but the head remains available for the first cleanup step. Paper, body, copies, and prior evidence are unaffected.
- **Extras:** one Wound incapacitates; after-combat survival is adjudicated under SWADE p.95.

### Difficulty review

- **Expected pressure:** one physically capable Extra, one weak combatant, environmental choice pressure, and simultaneous evidence goals.
- **Volatility or swing risk:** moderate for a Vigor d4 solo Wild Card; reduce by having Ray flee/surrender and by making Cal prioritize the truck/log over attacks.
- **Resource drain:** Bennies, possible Wounds, evidence categories, and custody.
- **Player-count adjustment:** written only for one Novice Wild Card. Do not increase opposition.
