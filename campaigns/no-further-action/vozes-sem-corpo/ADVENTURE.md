# Vozes sem Corpo

English-language preparation for night 2 of *No Further Action*. The Portuguese title is retained. This is preparation, not session history. The [Grounding audit](QUALITY.md#grounding-audit) passed 2026-09-19 after GM-approved values; rebuild `RUN.md` before table use.

## One-sentence premise

The morning after Chief Whitley shelves Frank Loman, Lilly reaches 102.3 FM while a purported engineer is already trying to take Abigail's reel, and she must find Frank and preserve enough independent evidence to prevent the file from being honestly dismissed as a drowning before the crew moves his body and erases the originals.

## GM intent

Deliver a 3–5 hour solo investigation in which ordinary tape, timing, town memory, and paperwork explain an apparently impossible voice. Keep about 70% of play investigative and 30% action. Make Ashgrove's smallness operational: unfamiliar men and an out-of-county truck generate witnesses, timing, and pressure. End with a single compound climax whose objectives are preserving Frank and an original record, not merely defeating two men. Reveal a repeatable synchronization gap and a local crime, not a national organization.

## Player-facing mission

Hear Abigail's tape, find Frank Loman, and build a file that cannot honestly be dismissed as a drowning.

## Objectives and stakes

### Main objective

Determine what happened to Frank and preserve independently supportable evidence before cleanup lets the file be honestly dismissed as a drowning.

### Secondary objectives

- Keep an original recording or authenticated copy in police custody.
- Establish the identical missing interval across independent records.
- Preserve the body, compound log, or signal equipment through the climax.
- Identify Ray and Cal through witnesses and local access records.

### Stakes

- **If Lilly acts quickly:** she can secure the reel, exploit the town's fresh memory, reach Frank before movement, and preserve an original signal record.
- **If Lilly delays without alerting the crew:** witnesses become less precise and Cal gets closer to loading Frank, but he does not begin destroying records.
- **If Lilly alerts the crew or then delays:** Ray warns Cal, active destruction begins, and Whitley gains a reason to call the work speculative.
- **If Lilly fails:** the crew may move Frank and erase the cleanest originals, but protected copies, dispatch audio, the cassette, witnesses, and local records preserve partial truth.

## Starting state

Lilly's municipal house is **14 Cedar** if she starts from home; Town Hall / Police is a short walk north of Main. **KCRK** is a short drive south across the Bridge Street bridge, on the Trapper Ridge side of the river. Abigail has refused to release the original reel, so he is still inside asking for it when Lilly reaches the studio later; Lilly's exact arrival time stays open. If Lilly arrives without alerting him, all evidence categories remain and she may observe or intervene before active cleanup. The adventure's single reversal occurs when Cal receives warning or unmistakably detects police attention: he begins active destruction and movement.
