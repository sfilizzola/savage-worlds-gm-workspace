# Inland tower compound

- **Canon status:** established as the place Lilly found Frank and the shooting happened (Session 2)
- **Region/time period:** Inland from Lake Barrow, off the lake road (Barrow Lake Rd / Co. Rd. 12), January 1986. **Not** KCRK. **Not** the north–south powerline easement printed on Sheet 2. Do not draw this site on the public 1984 sheets.
- **Function:** Utility tower / outbuilding compound. Worked as a crime scene. Two outsiders were moving Frank's body to a truck here when Lilly arrived.
- **First impression:** Wet metal, a tower you can climb, a truck being loaded, then gunfire. Afterward: a processed scene, ambulance gone, paper to write.
- **Persistent truths:**
  - Frank Loman's body was here, not in the lake, in the act of being transported to the truck.
  - Ray Holtz died here. Cal Briggs was shot here and died in the ambulance.
  - Lilly climbed the tower and felt a little **nausea**, which **stopped** as if equipment had been shut down once the police situation was obvious.
  - Fake / inapt paperwork got the men access to work around the lake. Purpose of that work is **not** established.
- **Current state:** Scene processed by Lilly and Doyle. File closed. Site is not a public landmark.
- **People/factions:** Ray and Cal (dead). No named local owner of the job.
- **Resources and hazards:** Height, wet ground, leftover gear if a later night returns. Nausea was present until the site “turned off.”
- **Secrets:** GM — local Concordance capture/replay job in prep; **not** player-known. Nausea mechanism undecided (see [`../lore/the-concordance.md`](../lore/the-concordance.md)).
- **How it changes if ignored:** The signed death file still exists. The public maps still omit the compound.
- **Historical/background sources:** none
- **Maps/handouts:** none player-facing. Never draw on [`ashgrove_map_1984.png`](ashgrove_map_1984.png) or [`ashgrove_town_map_1984.png`](ashgrove_town_map_1984.png).
