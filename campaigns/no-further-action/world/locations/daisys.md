# Daisy’s

- **Canon status:** established (Session 2; breakfast witnesses for the two outsiders)
- **Region/time period:** South side of Main Street / ORE. 328, first numbered door east of Church Street; January 1986–
- **Function:** Town living room. Breakfast, night-shift coffee, Walt Kearney’s booth.
- **First impression:** Climate / See / Hear / Feel — damp coats, a Cinder Creek clock, pie under a dome, the register bell, coffee that has been on the burner too long.
- **Persistent truths:**
  - **Daisy Pell** (~60, mill-widow) works the register at breakfast.
  - Booths. Locals have unofficial seats. Outsiders get remembered. Session 2: Daisy’s was essential to placing Ray and Cal.
  - Walt Kearney at about 7am. Kevin Alder may come through about 2am if he is actually working the beat.
- **Current state:** Open. Daisy is angry that some of Frank’s friends blamed lake nausea on her food.
- **People/factions:** Daisy Pell; whoever walks in (PD, mill families, mayor on a good day).
- **Resources and hazards:** Gossip that is human, not oracular. Anyone in a booth can see who meets whom. The food-poisoning rumor is false in Lilly’s judgment.
- **Secrets:** none established
- **How it changes if ignored:** Coffee still gets poured. Walt still sits. The rumor may still travel.
- **Historical/background sources:** none
