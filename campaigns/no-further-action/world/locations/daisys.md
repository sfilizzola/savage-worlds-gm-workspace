# Daisy’s

- **Canon status:** established (Session 2; breakfast witnesses for the two outsiders). The Owl-overflow relationship and the working May 1986 state below are **authored as true for this table; not yet in play**.
- **Region/time period:** South side of Main Street / ORE. 328, first numbered door east of Church Street; January 1986–
- **Function:** Town living room. Breakfast, night-shift coffee, Walt Kearney’s booth.
- **First impression:** Climate / See / Hear / Feel — damp coats, a Cinder Creek clock, pie under a dome, the register bell, coffee that has been on the burner too long.
- **Persistent truths:**
  - **Daisy Pell** (~60, mill-widow) works the register at breakfast.
  - Booths. Locals have unofficial seats. Outsiders get remembered. Session 2: Daisy’s was essential to placing Ray and Cal.
  - Walt Kearney at about 7am. [Kevin Alder](../npcs/kevin-alder.md) may come through about 2am if he is actually working the beat.
  - Same numbered south-side row as [The Hoot Owl](the-hoot-owl.md), three doors west of it past Iverson’s Market and Pell’s Hardware. When the pub is shut, full, or holding a private booking, its crowd walks down to Daisy’s, because there is no other Main Street door to sit down behind after dark. **Wednesday and Saturday** — the mill crowd’s drinking nights — are normally her quiet evenings.
  - [Daisy Pell](../npcs/daisy-pell.md) is [Len Pell](../npcs/len-pell.md)’s sister-in-law. News about the mill yard reaches town across her counter before it reaches the precinct.
- **Current state:** Open. Daisy is angry that some of Frank’s friends blamed lake nausea on her food. Working May 1986 state (**authored, not history**): on the night of the Owl’s private supper the diner is jammed with displaced drinkers well past its usual hour.
- **People/factions:** Daisy Pell; whoever walks in (PD, mill families, mayor on a good day).
- **Resources and hazards:** Gossip that is human, not oracular. Anyone in a booth can see who meets whom. The food-poisoning rumor is false in Lilly’s judgment.
- **Secrets:** none established
- **How it changes if ignored:** Coffee still gets poured. Walt still sits. The rumor may still travel.
- **Historical/background sources:** none
