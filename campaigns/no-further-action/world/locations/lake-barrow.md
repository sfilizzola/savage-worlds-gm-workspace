# Lake Barrow

- **Canon status:** established as a place Lilly worked in January 1986
- **Region/time period:** About 5 km from Ashgrove by the lake road; on the 1984 county sheet the water sits **southwest** of the town grid (printed **Barrow Lake** = this lake). January 1986
- **Function:** Fishing water / local lake. Site of the Loman scene.
- **First impression:** Fog after dark; water at the end of tracks; metal-adjacent sound from across the water. During the gap, people close to the water felt nauseated.
- **Persistent truths:**
  - Local fishing water **southwest** of Ashgrove, about 5 km by **Barrow Lake Rd** / Lake Barrow Rd (**Co. Rd. 12** on the campaign lock). **Clear Creek** feeds it. Timber to the water. **Barrow Bait & Tackle** on the road, not in town. A few cabins if drawn (**Barrow Landing**) are not a municipality.
  - Loman’s car was on the road to the lake. Prints (shod and barefoot) reached the water and stopped.
  - Cassette in the mud by the car. Car radio: metallic static, distorted voice; later seemed to whisper Lilly’s name.
  - Frank was **not** in the lake. Body recovered inland (see [`inland-tower-compound.md`](inland-tower-compound.md)).
- **Current state:** Loman death file closed. Lake friends who were out at the gap also felt nausea; some blamed Daisy’s.
- **People/factions:** Loman fished here. Doyle was at the night-1 scene. Helen is Frank’s sister.
- **Resources and hazards:** Water, fog, isolation. Nausea during the night-1 gap for people close to the lake. Cause unset.
- **Secrets:** Purpose of the inland tower work is not established. Do not write ClearWave onto the shoreline.
- **How it changes if ignored:** The water keeps its silence; the signed file lives at the precinct.
- **Historical/background sources:** none
