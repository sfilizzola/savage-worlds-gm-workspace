# The Hoot Owl

- **Canon status:** authored as true for this table; not yet in play
- **Region/time period:** South side of Main Street / ORE. 328, numbered row between Pell's Hardware and the leftover Ashgrove Arms (Sheet 2); 1986–2016.
- **Function:** Town pub through 2016.
- **First impression:** Rain beads on the dark front windows and shines under the Main Street lamps. Inside, a long bar, close-set tables, and old mill photographs hold the warm smell of beer and fried onions; talk rises over a radio behind the bar, and the door opens often enough to admit a stripe of wet pavement.
- **Persistent truths:**
  - **Pat Calhoun** owns The Hoot Owl.
  - Wednesday and Saturday are the mill-crowd drinking nights.
  - **Len Pell** is a regular on those nights.
  - A radio can be playing diegetically when the pub is open.
- **Current state:** Working May 1986 state, not history — tonight the Owl is closed to walk-ins for **Oscar Lind's** mill old-shift send-off supper; overflow goes to Daisy's.
- **People/factions:** Pat Calhoun; Len Pell and the mill crowd on Wednesdays and Saturdays; Oscar Lind's old-shift supper party tonight.
- **Resources and hazards:** A familiar Main Street door, a bar radio, and a crowd whose routines are common town knowledge. A private booking pushes regulars elsewhere.
- **Secrets:** None involving the Concordance. GM — **Marnie Quade** sometimes passes an envelope here; she does not know the mill job.
- **How it changes if ignored:** It stays the Main Street pub; a later plaza bar is a different place.
