# Earl Voss

- **Canon status:** authored as true for this table; not yet in play
- **Visibility:** mixed — town knows he sleeps; Lilly learns it if she works nights or listens
- **Role:** Night patrol, Ashgrove PD, 56. The sleeper.
- **Current objective:** Get through the night. “Nothing happens after eleven.”
- **Fear:** A real call while he is down. Whitley actually caring. Len Pell making the mill lot a problem.
- **Leverage/resources:** Seniority, the cruiser, mill-lot habit.
- **Secret:** Open secret, not a file: he sleeps. Whitley does not care if headlines stay down. He is **not** paid and **not** in on anything — no envelope, no arrangement, nothing he could confess. He does not know his own postings are useful to anyone, and he would be insulted by the suggestion.
- **Relationships:** **Kevin Alder** actually runs the beat. **Len Pell** on the mill yard — Earl’s nap is a relationship. Not graft in the Whitley sense; just checked out after the mill died.
- **The West End posting** (authored as true for this table; not yet in play): on **Wednesday and Saturday** nights — the nights Len drinks at [The Hoot Owl](../locations/the-hoot-owl.md) — the chief’s sheet puts Earl on West End and the [mill](../locations/cinder-creek-lumber.md). Earl reads that as the easy half of the town and takes it gladly. It is a hole in the beat, not a favor he was asked for.
- **The nest** (same status): he backs the cruiser into the open bay of the empty scale house just inside the yard fence, out of the wind and off the West End street, nose pointed at the gate. Heater until the glass fogs, then engine off, window cracked, radio turned down to a mutter. Asleep from roughly **midnight to 5 a.m.**, when the cold wakes him and he drives a lap before shift end. Anyone looking for Earl for any reason can find the car; it is twenty yards of gravel off the fence line and it has been the same spot for two years.
- **If ignored:** He keeps sleeping. Kevin keeps driving.
- **What can change their behavior:** A body, a chief in the lot, Kevin refusing to cover, Len banging the glass.

## Portrayal

- First impression: Tired mill years in the face. Already sitting like the shift is over.
- Voice/manner cue: Short. Weather. “Call if it’s real.”
- Spoken lines:
  - “Nothing happens after eleven.”
  - “Kevin’s out. I’m here.”
- Avoid: Comic relief only, a sudden action-hero turn, briefing Lilly on the campaign.
- Pressure variants:
  - Cooperate: Gives the one bounded fact his mill posting lets him know. “Len's truck was in before I backed into the scale house. That's all I saw.”
  - Refuse: Eyes closed again. “Write it in the morning.”
  - Threatened: Sits up angry and procedural, then wants it to be Kevin’s problem.
  - Ignored: Sleeps. Radio low.
