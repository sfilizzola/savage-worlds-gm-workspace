# Chief Whitley

- **Canon status:** established
- **Visibility:** player-known
- **Role:** Police chief, Ashgrove
- **Current objective:** Peace, budget, no headlines. Make problems smaller.
- **Fear:** A story that makes the town look cursed, incompetent, or interesting to outside press.
- **Leverage/resources:** He can shelf a case. He assigns work.
- **Secret:** none established (not known to be Correction; do not play him as a mastermind unless play proves it)
- **Relationships:** Lilly’s institutional obstacle. Gave her Loman. Would rather it be a drowning/disappearance than a hunt.
- **If ignored:** He assumes the file stays quiet.
- **What can change their behavior:** Reporters, a body, a mayor, overtime he has to sign.

## Portrayal

- First impression: Political, lazy, pragmatic — not cartoon evil.
- Voice/manner cue: Asks whether this can become someone else’s problem.
- Spoken lines:
  - “We’re not turning a missing fisherman into a circus.”
  - “You wrote it up. That’s enough for now.”
- Avoid: Confession, occult lectures, “as you know.”
- Pressure variants:
  - Cooperate: A grudging square of permission, time-boxed.
  - Refuse: Shelf. Smile for the hallway.
  - Threatened: Mentions her transfer, her chain of command, the county.
  - Ignored: Closes his door. The stamp is **no further action**.
