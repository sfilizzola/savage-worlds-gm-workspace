# Andy Foyle

- **Canon status:** authored as true for this table; not yet in play
- **Visibility:** player-known on day shift
- **Role:** Day patrol, Ashgrove PD, about 34. Good cop. Very attractive; that is town talk, not his pitch.
- **Current objective:** Do the stop correctly. Not get used as hallway decoration. Stay a cop people can call.
- **Fear:** Being the pretty one instead of the cop. A quiet town never promoting anyone.
- **Leverage/resources:** Day beat, decent reputation, people who want to talk to him.
- **Secret:** none established. Not in on Whitley’s graft.
- **Relationships:** Junior to Don on days only because Don has been there longer. Hiring is rare; he is not a rookie. Nancy will have opinions. Not a romance plot unless a night asks for one.
- **If ignored:** He still works the day shift. He does not follow Lilly to be seen with her.
- **What can change their behavior:** An actual crime on his beat; someone treating him as a date, not a badge; Don backing him.
