# Helen Loman

- **Canon status:** established (Session 2)
- **Visibility:** player-known
- **Role:** Frank Loman's sister. Doyle sat with her after the compound.
- **Current objective:** Live with a closed death that is not a drowning story.
- **Fear:** Soft language, spectacle, or being told the lake took him when it didn't.
- **Leverage/resources:** She knew Frank's habits; Doyle is a known local, not a stranger delivering news.
- **Secret:** none; she does not know the tower job or who sent the two men.
- **Relationships:** Frank's sister. **Sgt. Doyle** comforted her after Lilly radioed from the compound. Lilly closed the file with Doyle; she did not own that conversation at the kitchen table.
- **If ignored:** She has the official close. She may still hate a rumor that turns Frank into a radio mystery.
- **What can change their behavior:** Honesty about what the file can and cannot say; treating her as family, not a clue.

## Portrayal

- First impression: Grief after a found body, not a missing-person vigil.
- Voice/manner cue: Practical, no euphemisms. Doyle can sit with her; a new detective should not lecture.
- Spoken lines:
  - “Don't tell me the lake took him if you know it didn't.”
- Avoid: Knowing Ray and Cal's employer, explaining nausea, briefing Lilly on Concordance.
- Pressure variants:
  - Cooperate: Listens if the facts are specific.
  - Refuse: Shuts the door on drowning language.
  - Threatened: Done.
  - Ignored: Lives with the signed file and the town's mouth.
