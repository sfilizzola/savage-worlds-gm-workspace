# Locations and Situations

Locations provide actionable state, not predetermined solutions.

## <Location Name>

- **Role in adventure:**
- **First impression:** four labeled lines the GM can paraphrase to the table (Climate / See / Hear / Feel). Player-perceivable only. Compiles into story-point **Mood**. No secrets, unearned names, historical footnotes, or “this is not the X.”
- **Current situation:** what is happening when the PCs arrive.
- **Environment:** terrain, light, weather, cover, access, hazards.
- **People/forces present:** links or concise summaries.
- **What can be learned:** observations, evidence, testimony, records.
- **Spoken lines:** one to four quoted lines if someone here can talk. Omit if nobody speaks.
- **Discoverable rolls:** Trait, modifier, fail, success, raise for each fact that can be learned here.
- **GM-only truth:**
- **What changes over time:**
- **If violence begins:** participants, goals, reinforcement, retreat, collateral risk.
- **Relevant mechanics:** verified source pointers and short paraphrases.
- **Links:** related story points, NPCs, maps, handouts, and sources.

Do not list a menu of player approaches. Supply enough state to adjudicate approaches the players invent.
