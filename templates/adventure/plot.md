## Story Point 1 - <Name>

- **Purpose:** why this point matters to the main plot.
- **Reachable when:** conditions that make it available.
- **Situation:** people, forces, and pressures currently present.
- **Spoken lines:** quoted lines if someone here speaks. Omit if nobody speaks.
- **Essential information:** what the players may need to learn.
- **Discoverable rolls:** Trait, modifier, fail, success, raise for each essential fact.
- **Independent vectors:** at least two or three people, places, observations, records, or consequences that can reveal essential information.
- **Escalation:** what changes if the PCs hesitate or leave.
- **Failure consequences:** costs and changed circumstances that preserve meaningful forward play.
- **Links forward:** story points that become reachable; not a mandatory next scene.
- **Optional/essential:** essential | optional.

Copy the section for each story point.
